## Key dated changes
- **MyHeritage:** uploads closed in stages from about 22 May 2025, fully by early August 2025 (company help page, July 2026). Since late 2025 new kits are low-pass whole-genome (~2x); old kits are not recalculated.
- **AncestryDNA:** from January 2026 the raw file is reduced (about 677k → 435k lines; Y and mtDNA removed). Not confirmed by the company; reported independently by the h600 file-format wiki, DNA-Explained comments and forum users.
- **FamilyTreeDNA:** Family Finder moved to NGS on 2 March 2026. Upload unlock ($19) retired 18 August 2026; uploads get free matches and Matrix; browser and ethnicity via a $29 upgrade. New Ancestry and MyHeritage WGS files are refused.
- **23andMe:** bankruptcy (March 2025), assets bought by TTAM (closed 14 July 2025), about 1.9 million deletion requests. Advanced DNA Comparison (segments) back from 4 March 2026 for Premium only.
- **Genotek:** forum reports from mid-2026 that new FTDNA, MyHeritage, Ancestry and Atlas files are not accepted; whole-genome files are accepted free.
