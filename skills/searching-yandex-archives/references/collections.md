# What is in Yandex Archive Search

Read this before the first query for a new region or type of source: it shows whether the person you need could be in the service at all. The contents keep growing; check against the "Archive" filter in the results themselves. The lists below are as of October 2026.

## Volume (the service's help page, October 2026)
- more than 23 million sheets of archival documents of the 18th to early 20th century;
- about 6 million pages of periodicals and directories of the mid-19th to early 21st century.

## Archive section
Archives of more than twenty regions of Russia (per search results: "archives of 20 regions" in 2024-2025; new ones have been added since; the exact number is shown by the "Archive" filter). Found in the results and descriptions:
- **TsGA Moskvy** (Central State Archive of Moscow): metrical books of Moscow churches, confession lists, revision lists; inventories of personal files of Soviet institutions;
- **TsGAMO** (Central State Archive of the Moscow Region): metrical books and confession lists of the uezds of the guberniya;
- archives of the **Astrakhan, Orenburg, Irkutsk, Omsk** oblasts, **Sevastopol** and others.

### Underrated: lists of evacuees in Orenburg (Chkalov), 1942–1943
The United State Archive of Orenburg Oblast (fond R-2144): "Lists of evacuated citizens accommodated in the … district of the city of Chkalov" («Списки эвакуированных граждан, размещённых в … районе г. Чкалова»), by city district, 1942–1943. Columns: surname, given name, patronymic, relation to the head of the family, sex, age, nationality, where from (oblast, city), occupation and length of service; the rows of one family are grouped under a family number, and there is an address in Chkalov.
- **A name index to all the lists** (inventory A, file 0, about 1,600 scans): surname, given name, patronymic → file and folio. Start with it: it shows at once how many people with this surname were in the city, and it leads to the right folio.
- These lists are **not in the Yad Vashem database**: a zero there means nothing for people evacuated to Chkalov.
- The recognised text of handwritten tables mixes up columns and letters, especially the first letter of a given name: read age and relationship from the scan.

Types of documents: metrical books, confession lists (*ispovednye vedomosti*), revision lists, censuses, clergy lists (*klirovye vedomosti*).

**Not included:** archives of Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, Poland, the Baltic states. For these places a zero in the archive section is a property of the service.

When Moscow metrical books are useful to people who were not from Moscow: students, merchants, military men, doctors, retired soldiers and their families registered marriages, births and deaths at their place of stay. An entry in a Moscow metrical book often names the **place of registration**, which is the key to the guberniya archive.

## Periodicals
- ***Kommersant*** (1909-1917): provincial chronicle, obituaries, trade news, bankruptcies;
- **"Senate announcements"** (*Senatskie obyavleniya*): auctions, mortgages of property, summonses to heirs, searches, with full names;
- guberniya gazettes (*gubernskie vedomosti*) of a number of guberniyas;
- Soviet newspapers: *Vechernyaya Moskva*, *Pravda*, *Izvestiya* and others;
- ***Russky Invalid*** (*Русский инвалид*, "The Russian Invalid"): among other things it printed **lists of men wounded in the Russo-Japanese War of 1904–1905** [verify whether the lists of the killed and missing are also in the results]. Search them by surname in the periodicals section; the second identifier is the regiment, rank and given name. Useful when there are no other documents about a soldier's service.

## Books and directories
- *Vsya Moskva* ("All Moscow"; various years, including the 1920s);
- *Vsya Rossiya* ("All Russia");
- *Rossiiskii meditsinskii spisok* (Russian Medical List);
- criminal-record registers (*vedomosti sudimosti*), casualty lists of 1914-1917.

⚠️ In directories the recognition confuses columns: check the heading against the scan.

## The full-size page image
The viewer and the preview give about 800 px: small handwriting and the figures in tables (age, family number, folio) cannot be read at that size. The page gets the full image (about 2,200 px) from the server through a temporary permission for the specific image; how to do this in the same tab and what must not be saved is in `SKILL.md`, section 3, item "The full scan". Take the quotation for a finding from the full image, not from the preview.

## What to use instead when the service is unavailable or the region is missing
| What you need | Where to go |
|---|---|
| Directories of the Russian Empire | Genealogy Indexer (in Cyrillic), archive.org (text files of volumes), GPIB (elib.shpl.ru, State Public Historical Library) |
| Pre-revolutionary newspapers | gpa.eastview.com/crl/irn (newspapers of the Russian Empire, full text), Google Books with a phrase in pre-reform orthography |
| Jewish press | National Library of Israel: skill `searching-nli-jewish-press` |
| Metrical books of Ukraine | Wikimedia Commons: skill `ukrainian-archives-on-commons` |
| Metrical books of Moldova | FamilySearch films: skill `searching-familysearch-films` |
