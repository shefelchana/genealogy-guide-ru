## Techniques

- Search for the **shtetl** in all spellings: Russian, Polish, Yiddish, modern Ukrainian or Lithuanian. In YIVO the town index already links the variants.
- **Studio**: look for the stamp on the back in the "Great Russian Album" and in FotoTikon (see `dating-old-photos`) — other families may have dated photographs from the same studio with the same backdrop.
- Compare **clothing** with photographs of the same region and decade, not with Hasidic photographs from New York or Jerusalem.
- If you find a similar photograph, write down the link, the catalogue number and the caption **word for word**; it is comparative material, not proof that the same people are in your photograph.
