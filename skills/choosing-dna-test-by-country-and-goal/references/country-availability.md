## Laws
- **Russia — Federal law No. 43-FZ of 20 Feb 2026**, in force 1 Sep 2026: transfer of genetic and immunological research data abroad and to foreigners only by decision of an inter-agency commission (Government decree No. 750). Fines not found in sources. Application to a private person's own sample — not stated clearly; forum moderators treat sending samples abroad as forbidden. Sources: Pharmvestnik (6 May 2026); T-invariant (30 Jul 2026); VGD forum moderator posts (Sep–Oct 2026). The text of the law itself was not opened [verify against the law text].
- **Israel — Genetic Information Law 5761-2000**: genetic testing by physician referral or court order in approved labs. Sources disagree whether direct-to-consumer tests are banned or regulated. Calcalist (2020, possibly outdated); family-laws.co.il (undated).

## Disputed points (do not present as settled)
1. 23andMe in Israel: "yes" (The DNA Geek, Feb 2026) vs "does not ship to Israeli addresses" (a legal overview site, 2026).
2. Whether 43-FZ covers private persons — unclear; advise conservatively.
3. "Buy MyHeritage and you get FamilyTreeDNA too" — the MyHeritage WGS file is not accepted by FamilyTreeDNA as of Nov 2026.
4. Is Y-37 / Big Y still worth it vs whole genome 30x — open debate.
5. Did MyHeritage delete Russian users' accounts — forum claims both ways, nothing official.
