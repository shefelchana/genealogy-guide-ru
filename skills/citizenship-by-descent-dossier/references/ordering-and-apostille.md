# Where to order documents and who affixes the apostille

**Currency: 07.10.2026.** Procedures and fees change — the user checks on the authority's site before ordering. Ordering, payment and logging into portals are done by the user.

## General rules

1. **Archival reference first, then the order.** Find the record in a digitized metrical book, census, inventory or database and write down the archive, fond, inventory (*opis*), file (*delo*), folio, record number. An order "by reference" is carried out faster and more accurately than "find everything about my great-grandfather".
2. **What the authorities accept:** repeat certificates of ZAGS/DRATS (civil registry office), archival certificates and archival copies with a seal. Not accepted: uncertified photocopies, printouts from databases (JewishGen, Geni, MyHeritage), family trees.
3. **The apostille is affixed by the country that issued the document**, and only on documents of its own bodies. A document of a Ukrainian archive is apostilled in Ukraine, of a Russian ZAGS — in Russia.
4. **Translation comes after the apostille**, by a sworn translator of the country of filing or by the consul (Poland). Germany accepts only a sworn translation.
5. **Record not found** — request a "certificate of absence of a record". The authority sees that a search was done, and the chain can be closed with another document.
6. **Ask for the necessary fields.** For Israel — the nationality of the parents in the repeat certificate; for all — the full certificate with the parents' names, not a short extract.

## Ukraine
- Repeat certificates (from the 1920s) — DRATS bodies.
- Metrical records before the 1920s — oblast state archives (DAKhmO — State Archive of Khmelnytskyi Oblast, DAKO — State Archive of Kyiv Oblast, DAViO — State Archive of Vinnytsia Oblast, and others); civil-status records of the Soviet period have also partly been transferred to oblast archives.
- The apostille on **archival** documents has been affixed since 19.09.2023 by **Ukrderzharkhiv** (State Archival Service); on documents of the justice bodies (including DRATS) — by the Ministry of Justice, about 610 UAH, usually 2 working days (guide.diia.gov.ua).

## Moldova
- The National Archives Agency issues certificates of birth, marriage, death from the metrical records of **1820–1924**, from 58 lei; application online through servicii.gov.md (arhiva.gov.md).
- Records of 1925–1940 (the Romanian period) — **not verified against the page**: according to search results — Arhiva Națională or Agenția Servicii Publice.
- Germany accepts multilingual extracts of Moldova without legalization (BVA leaflet).

## Russia
- The apostille on ZAGS documents — regional ZAGS bodies; on archival certificates — the archives (or the authorized body of the region).
- State fee — 2,500 RUB for each document (subpara. 48, para. 1, Art. 333.33 of the Tax Code of the RF, checked 07.10.2026). The procedure in a particular ZAGS or archive — **not verified against the page**.

## Belarus
- The procedure of apostille and ordering — **not verified against the page**. Tell the user that this needs to be clarified with the ZAGS or archive of Belarus.

## Countries of filing — where the documents on the ancestor's status lie
- **Germany:** Meldekarten and address books of cities (city archives), compensation files.
- **Poland:** Polish passports and identity cards of ancestors; civil-status records — Polish USC (civil registry offices) and state archives.
- **Lithuania:** LVIA (metrical records before 1940).
- **Latvia:** the 1935 census, pre-war passports, tax and conscription files.
- **Romania:** Romanian civil-status records of 1918–1940 for Bessarabia and Bukovina.

## What to tell the user if the document does not exist

- Suggest **indirect documents for the same link**: the metrical record of a brother or sister with the same parents, a census, a family list (*posemeinyi spisok*), a military booklet, a work book with the date and place of birth.
- Check whether there is **a file of a relative** who has already gone through the procedure (the BVA pulls a file by number).
- If a link rests only on family tradition, say so: for the authority this link is not yet proved.
