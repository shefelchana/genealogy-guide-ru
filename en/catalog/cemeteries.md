# Cemeteries and gravestones

What this section covers: burial databases and photographs of gravestones. On the Hebrew side of a Jewish gravestone there is usually the father's name ("ben …" / "bat …") — often the only source of that name. Photograph both sides of the stone. Markers are in [README.md](README.md).

General rule: the coverage of all cemetery databases is **incomplete**. The absence of a record proves nothing.

---

### JOWBR — Jewish Online Worldwide Burial Registry (part of JewishGen)
- **What is there:** over 7 million burial records and 908,000 photos, about 9,500 cemeteries in 143 countries [checked]. Free, JewishGen login required.
- **Experience:** a grave in Bendery (1873–1927) confirmed the dates of a person's life from the metrical books. For other places the person was not found — the burial is not indexed.
- **For advanced users:** in the JewishGen search address — `allcountry=DEFAULT` or `CEMETERY`.

### "Mitzvat Emet" (mitzvatemet.com) (Russian-language interface)
- **What is there:** a photo catalog of burials in Belarus, Ukraine, Russia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan with photos of the monuments.
- **Access:** Free, no registration, browser only (Cloudflare).
- **Limit:** **reCAPTCHA after about 5 searches**: the form silently stops submitting and shows no errors. Do not bypass it — repeat later, **one search per visit**.
- **How to search:** enter the **beginning** of the surname, **choose the suggestion** with the mouse, click "Search" (Поиск). The suggestion shows whether the surname is in the catalog at all. Results are 20 per page; each entry has a town, cemetery, photo.
- **Traps:** a chat opens by itself on the page — do not type into it; direct links to photos are closed.

### Toldot (toldot.com/life/cemetery) (Russian-language)
- **What is there:** Jewish cemeteries of Russia and Ukraine. Search by **substring** ("Shefel" also finds "Shefelman"), 20 records per page.
- **Coverage is selective:** well-known gravestones are sometimes not found in the database.
- **For advanced users:** addressed by a link `…/cemetery/?show_result=1&last_name=X&…`, paging by the parameter `curPos=20,40…`.

### Find a Grave (findagrave.com)
- Photographs and data of American and other cemeteries; memorial pages are often connected with links from FamilySearch indexes. The stone sometimes has the Hebrew name of the father. A memorial has a button to request a photograph by a volunteer (see [Requests and letters](../guide/2-reference/20-requests-letters.md)).

### "Stone Archive" ("Kamennyi arkhiv") on j-roots
- A cemetery database on the "Evreiskie korni" (Jewish Roots) forum; available only with a (free) forum login. See [forums-and-communities.md](forums-and-communities.md). (Russian-language)

### IAJGS Cemetery Project (iajgscemetery.org)
- The project of the International Association of Jewish Genealogical Societies on Jewish cemeteries; free (mentioned on iajgs.org [checked], the project's own site was not verified).

### Bessarabia SIG: cemetery project
- Over 90 cemeteries (from search results), about 75 thousand burials in Bessarabia [page: jewishgen.org/bessarabia, 2026-10-07]. jewishgen.org/bessarabia.

### Landsmanshaftn societies at US cemeteries
- A free database of plots of landsmanshaftn (hometown societies) from almost 100 cemeteries in the New York area (over 10,600 records) — at JGSNY [page: jgsny.org, 08.10.2026]. The burial society points to the home town. See [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md).
