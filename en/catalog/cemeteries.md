# Cemeteries and gravestones

What this section covers: databases of burials and photographs of gravestones. On the Hebrew side of a Jewish gravestone there is usually the father's name ("ben …" / "bat …") — often the only source of that name. Photograph both sides of the stone. Markers are explained in [README.md](README.md).

General rule: the coverage of all cemetery databases is **incomplete**. The absence of a record proves nothing.

---

### JOWBR — Jewish Online Worldwide Burial Registry (part of JewishGen) (English interface)
- **What is there:** more than 7 million burial records and 908,000 photos, about 9,500 cemeteries in 143 countries [checked]. Free, a JewishGen login is needed.
- **Experience:** a grave in Bender (1873–1927) confirmed the years of life of a person known from metrical books. For other places the person was not found — the burial is not indexed.
- **For advanced users:** in the JewishGen search address — `allcountry=DEFAULT` or `CEMETERY`.

### Mitzvat Emet (mitzvatemet.com)
- **What is there:** a photo catalog of burials in Belarus, Ukraine, Russia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan with photos of the monuments.
- **Access:** Free, no registration, only in a browser (Cloudflare).
- **Limit:** **reCAPTCHA after about 5 searches**: the form silently stops submitting and shows no errors. Do not get around it — repeat later, **one search per visit**.
- **How to search:** enter the **beginning** of the surname, **choose the suggestion** with the mouse, press "Search". The suggestion shows whether the surname is in the catalog at all. Results are 20 per page; a record has the town, the cemetery, the photo.
- **Traps:** a chat opens by itself on the page — do not type into it; direct links to photos are closed.

### Toldot (toldot.com/life/cemetery)
- **What is there:** Jewish cemeteries of Russia and Ukraine. Search is by **substring** ("Шефел" finds "Шефельман" too — the search is typed in Cyrillic), 20 records per page. Don't read Russian? See [For English speakers](../guide/0-start/08-for-english-speakers.md).
- **Coverage is selective:** known gravestones are sometimes not found in the database.
- **For advanced users:** addressed by a link `…/cemetery/?show_result=1&last_name=X&…`, paging by the parameter `curPos=20,40…`.

### Find a Grave (findagrave.com)
- Photographs and data of American and other cemeteries; memorial pages are often linked from FamilySearch index links. The stone sometimes carries the Hebrew name of the father. A memorial has a button to request a photograph from a volunteer (see [Requests and letters](../guide/2-reference/20-requests-letters.md)).

### The "Stone Archive" on j-roots
- A cemetery database on the Jewish Roots forum (Еврейские корни, j-roots); available only under a (free) forum login. See [forums-and-communities.md](forums-and-communities.md).

### IAJGS Cemetery Project (iajgscemetery.org)
- A project of the International Association of Jewish Genealogical Societies on Jewish cemeteries; free (mentioned on iajgs.org [checked], the project's own site was not checked).

### Bessarabia SIG: cemetery project
- More than 90 cemeteries (per search results), about 75,000 burials of Bessarabia [checked: jewishgen.org/bessarabia, 2026-10-07]. jewishgen.org/bessarabia.

### Landsmanshaften societies in US cemeteries
- A free database of the plots of *landsmanshaftn* (hometown societies) in almost 100 cemeteries of the New York area (more than 10,600 records) — at JGSNY [checked: jgsny.org, 08.10.2026]. The burial place points to the home town. See [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md).
