# USA and emigration: sources

What this section covers: where to look for the documents of emigrants — American databases, ports of arrival, departure lists of European ports, sources for Canada, Argentina, Palestine, South Africa and Australia. A step-by-step algorithm "from American documents to the shtetl" and the traps are in [Emigration](../guide/2-reference/13-emigration.md).

Markers: **[checked]** — read on the page (October 2026); **[from search snippet]** — from search results, to be verified; "not verified on the page". Prices and fees change.

### Missouri state death certificates (s1.sos.mo.gov/records/archives/archivesmvc/deathcertificates)
- Certificates with scans; **free, no registration**. Search: surname "Starts with" + year.
- The scan is a PDF in an old format; on screen it may look empty — open it with another program. For immigrants the parents in the boxes are often "Unknown".

### FamilySearch American collections and related databases
- Passenger lists of New York and Philadelphia, naturalization index cards, "Pennsylvania Civil Marriages 1677–1950" (license applications with the parents' names), censuses of 1930/1940/1950, draft cards of two wars, SSDI. In addition — the US database on JewishGen and the Ellis Island Foundation index.
- **Techniques:** 20 or more surname variants in "exact" and "fuzzy" modes; search by the **mother's maiden name and place of birth**; manifests have "going to" — a direct lead; verify the person against several documents.
- **Traps:** a look-alike surname in the index; a wrongly read year. **Read the frame, not the index.**
- **An unused lead:** the immigrant banks' books in Philadelphia (Philadelphia Jewish Archives, Temple University) give, by ticket order number, a form with the sender's address and the recipient's shtetl.

## 2. Departure lists and ports of Europe

- **Hamburg** — 1850–1934 (except 1915–1919). About 90% of emigrants from Eastern Europe went through Hamburg. The lists are direct and indirect (with a change of ship). They contain **each emigrant's home town and his relatives on the same ship**. In German. Ancestry — paid, full index; FamilySearch — images in FamilySearch centers, a partial index for 1877–1914 [page: FS Wiki]. Morse has Hamburg forms.
- **Bremen** — 738,102 records, mostly 1920–1939, plus 1907/08 and 1913/14. Old lists were destroyed from 1875, the rest burned in the Second World War. Free (passagierlisten.de) [checked].
- **Antwerp** — the Red Star Line archive, including passenger lists, is lost [checked]. The replacement — **police files on foreigners**: FelixArchief (1840–1930, all digitized [from search snippet]; the index on FamilySearch has errors — check against the image [checked]) and the national files in the State Archives in Brussels (1839 – September 1944; open after 100 years; after the First World War usually with a photo) [checked].
- **Rotterdam** — Holland-America Line lists in the city archive; the 1900–1920 index is free [from search snippet].
- **Libau (Liepāja)** — Russian American Line 1906–1914, over 500,000 passengers in all; most of the records are lost [checked]. Search through the American arrival.
- **Liverpool** — departure registers 1890–1960 in the UK National Archives, without an index [checked].

## 3. Other countries

| Country | Source | What it gives |
|---|---|---|
| **Canada** | Library and Archives Canada: passenger lists 1865–1935; entry across the US border 1908–1935; **"Immigrants from the Russian Empire, 1898–1922"** (documents of the Russian consulates; the series "Passport and Identity Papers" is digitized and free); immigrants from 1936 — through a request to IRCC [checked] | names and variants, photo, year of birth, religion |
| **Argentina** | CEMLA — arrivals in the port of Buenos Aires (over 4.4 million people; the site has a CAPTCHA) [from search snippet]; **JCA Passenger Lists** on JewishGen — settlers in Baron Hirsch's colonies [checked] | in JCA — the **place of origin in Europe** |
| **Palestine / Israel** | Israel State Archives — naturalization 1937–1947 (see [foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md)); IGRA — over 27,000 name changes from the Palestine Gazette 1917–1948, lists of the Third Aliyah [checked] | place and date of birth, former citizenship, family |
| **South Africa** | SA Jewish Rootsbank (University of Cape Town, Kaplan Centre): naturalizations, arrivals, about 15,000 families 1850–1950, free [checked]; naturalization registers of the Cape Colony 1903–1907 [from search snippet] | including natives of Lithuania, Latvia, Belarus |
| **Australia** | National Archives of Australia: naturalization from 1904 (Victoria — 1848–1903); RecordSearch → NameSearch [from search snippet] | date and ship of arrival, town of birth, family |
| **France, Great Britain, Belgium** | see [europe-by-country.md](europe-by-country.md) | naturalization, files on foreigners |
