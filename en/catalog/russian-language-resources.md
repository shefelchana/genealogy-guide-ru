# Russian-language resources

What this section covers: Russian archives, libraries, databases and projects in Russian (other than JewishGen and FamilySearch) — what is in them and how to work with them. Databases on the war and repression by region are in [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md).

Markers: **[checked]** — read on the page (October 2026); **[from search snippet]** — from search results, to be verified.

⚠️ **From abroad some Russian government sites do not open** (in October 2026 — RGB, NEB, RNB, the Presidential Library, "Prozhito", the Rosarkhiv portal, a number of regional archives, Gosuslugi). This is a network restriction, not a sign that the site has closed. These worked from abroad: Yandex, GPIB, archive.org. See [Access, CAPTCHAs, pace](../guide/2-reference/19-access-captchas-pace.md).

## Archives

- **Yandex "Archive Search" (Poisk po arkhivam)** (yandex.ru/archive) (Russian-language) — metrical books, confessional lists, revision lists of the 18th – early 20th century from the archives of Moscow and the Astrakhan, Orenburg, Irkutsk and other oblasts; a neural network recognized the handwritten text. "More than 23 million archive sheets and about 6 million pages of periodicals" plus directories [checked]. The search understands word forms and pre-reform spelling. A card with techniques — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md).
- **Federal archives:**
  - **RGADA** (Russian State Archive of Ancient Acts) — fond 350 (Landrat books and revision lists 1707–1792) is digitized, but can be viewed only in the reading room; the reading room is free [from search snippet];
  - **RGIA** (Russian State Historical Archive) — inventories are scanned, electronic orders through a personal account at rgia.su [from search snippet];
  - the unified portal of Rosarkhiv (online.archives.ru) — did not open.
- **EAIS (unified archival information system) and electronic reading rooms of regional archives** — remote viewing of inventories and files, more often paid. Examples: in the Lipetsk archive — 60 ₽ an hour, 110 ₽ for 2 hours; in Kuzbass part of the documents is in free open access after registration [checked]; tariffs of "100 ₽ per day, 620 ₽ for 7 days" turn up in search results (archive not identified) [from search snippet]. In detail — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md).
- **BLITZ** (St. Petersburg) — a paid search in the archives of Moscow and St. Petersburg; a preliminary search — $80 (per FamilySearch).

## Databases about people

- **"Otkrytyi spisok" (Open List)** (ru.openlist.wiki) — victims of political repression 1917–1991, 3,380,827 records, expanded on the wiki principle, free [checked].
- **"Bessmertnyi barak" (Immortal Barrack)** (bessmertnybarak.ru) — since 2015; places of birth and death, file numbers, photos, memoirs [page: Wikipedia]; the number of records from different sources is 2.4 to 3.35 million [from search snippet].
- **"Vozvrashchennye imena" (Returned Names)** (visz.nlr.ru, RNB) — memorial books of the repressed, the "Leningradskii martirolog" (Leningrad Martyrology), "Blokada. 1941–1944. Leningrad" [from search snippet].
- **"Pamyat naroda" (People's Memory)** (pamyat-naroda.ru, Ministry of Defence) — since 2015 it has combined OBD "Memorial" and "Podvig naroda" [page: Wikipedia]. Techniques — [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **"Doroga pamyati" (Road of Memory)** (1418museum.ru) — about 33–34 million war participants with photos; there are photos from personal files that are not in other databases [from search snippet].
- **"Posledniy adres" (Last Address)**, **"Bessmertnyi polk" (Immortal Regiment)** and others — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md).

## Libraries and texts

- **NEB** (National Electronic Library; rusneb.ru) — a catalog of over 41 million records, over 5 million electronic documents; protected editions — after login or in electronic reading rooms [from search snippet]. Access statuses — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).
- **RNB, electronic library** (National Library of Russia; nlr.ru/elibrary) — over 650 thousand copies; the "Gazety v Seti" (Newspapers on the Web) index — over 5,600 digitized newspapers [from search snippet].
- **RGB** (Russian State Library), **GPIB** (State Public Historical Library), **the Presidential Library**, **"Runiversum"** — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).
- **"Elektronnaya biblioteka istoricheskikh dokumentov" (Electronic Library of Historical Documents)** (docs.historyrussia.org) — since 2017, over 250,000 documents [search; the site did not open].
- **Russian Empire newspapers** (CRL / East View) — [press.md](press.md).
- **"Prozhito"** (prozhito.org, European University at St. Petersburg) — 9,009 authors, 660,000 diary entries of the 18th–20th centuries (spring 2025); search by dates, authors, place where the diary was kept [page: Wikipedia]. Useful for historical context ([Writing the family history](../guide/3-results/03-family-history.md)).

## Communities and consolidated lists

- **VGD** (vgd.ru), **j-roots**, the SVRT forum — [forums-and-communities.md](forums-and-communities.md).
- **Lost Russian Family**: a consolidated list of over 50 free databases (repression, war, emigration to Canada, vanished villages, emigré newspapers) — lostrussianfamily.wpcomstaging.com/free-databases/ [checked]. The site is in English, the databases are mostly in Russian.
- S. Lerner's presentation "Russian Language Internet Resources for Jewish Genealogy" (IAJGS 2024) — a list of Russian-language resources for Jewish genealogy ([../reading/genealogists-and-authors.md](../reading/genealogists-and-authors.md)).
