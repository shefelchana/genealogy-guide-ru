# Russian-language resources

What this section covers: Russian archives, libraries, databases and projects in the Russian language (apart from JewishGen and FamilySearch) — what is in them and how to work with them. Databases on the war and repression by region are in [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md). The sites in this file are in Russian unless noted; searches need Cyrillic. Don't read Russian? See [For English speakers](../guide/0-start/08-for-english-speakers.md).

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified] and others) are explained in the [Glossary](../guide/0-start/06-glossary.md).

⚠️ **From abroad some Russian government sites do not open** (in October 2026 — RGB, NEB, RNB, the Presidential Library, "Prozhito", the Rosarkhiv portal, a number of oblast archives, Gosuslugi). This is a network restriction, not a sign that the site has closed. Working from abroad: Yandex, GPIB, archive.org. See [Access, CAPTCHAs, pace](../guide/2-reference/19-access-captchas-pace.md).

## Archives

- **Yandex "Archive Search"** (yandex.ru/archive) — metrical books, confession lists, revision lists of the 18th to early 20th century from the archives of Moscow and the Astrakhan, Orenburg, Irkutsk and other oblasts; a neural network recognized the handwriting. "More than 23 million archive sheets and about 6 million pages of periodicals" plus directories [checked]. The search understands word forms and pre-reform spelling. A card with techniques — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md).
- **Federal archives:**
  - **RGADA** (Russian State Archive of Ancient Acts) — fond 350 (the Landrat books and revision lists of 1707–1792) is digitized, but can be viewed only in the reading room; the reading room is free [from search snippet];
  - **RGIA** (Russian State Historical Archive) — the inventories are scanned, electronic orders through a personal account at rgia.su [from search snippet];
  - the single Rosarkhiv portal (online.archives.ru) — did not open on the date of checking.
- **EAIS and the electronic reading rooms of oblast archives** — remote viewing of inventories and files, more often paid. Examples: in the Lipetsk archive — 60 ₽ per hour, 110 ₽ for 2 hours; in Kuzbass part of the documents is freely available after registration [checked]; search results show tariffs of "100 ₽ per day, 620 ₽ for 7 days" (archive not identified) [from search snippet]. In detail — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md).
- **BLITZ** (St Petersburg) — paid search in the archives of Moscow and St Petersburg; a preliminary search costs $80 (per FamilySearch).

## Databases of people

- **"Open List"** (Открытый список) (ru.openlist.wiki) — victims of political repression 1917–1991, 3,380,827 records, grows on the wiki principle, free [checked].
- **"Immortal Barrack"** (Бессмертный барак) (bessmertnybarak.ru) — since 2015; places of birth and death, file numbers, photos, memoirs [checked: Wikipedia]; the number of records is from 2.4 to 3.35 million depending on the source [from search snippet].
- **"Returned Names"** (Возвращённые имена) (visz.nlr.ru, RNB) — memorial books of the repressed, the "Leningrad Martyrology", "Siege. 1941–1944. Leningrad" [from search snippet].
- **"People's Memory"** (Память народа) (pamyat-naroda.ru, Ministry of Defence) — since 2015 it has combined OBD "Memorial" and "Feat of the People" [checked: Wikipedia]. Techniques — [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **"Road of Memory"** (Дорога памяти) (1418museum.ru) — about 33–34 million participants of the war with photos; there are photos from personal files that are in no other database [from search snippet].
- **"Last Address"** (Последний адрес), **"Immortal Regiment"** (Бессмертный полк) and others — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md).

## Libraries and texts

- **NEB** (National Electronic Library) (rusneb.ru) — a catalog of more than 41 million records, more than 5 million electronic documents; protected editions after login or in electronic reading rooms [from search snippet]. Access statuses — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).
- **RNB, electronic library** (National Library of Russia, nlr.ru/elibrary) — more than 650 thousand copies; the "Newspapers on the Net" (Газеты в Сети) index — more than 5,600 digitized newspapers [from search snippet].
- **RGB** (Russian State Library), **GPIB** (Public Historical Library), **the Presidential Library**, **"Runiverse"** (Руниверс) — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).
- **"Electronic Library of Historical Documents"** (Электронная библиотека исторических документов) (docs.historyrussia.org) — since 2017, more than 250,000 documents [from search snippet].
- **Newspapers of the Russian Empire** (CRL / East View) — [press.md](press.md).
- **"Prozhito"** (prozhito.org, European University at St Petersburg) — 9,009 authors, 660,000 diary entries of the 18th–20th centuries (spring 2025); search by dates, authors, place where the diary was kept [checked: Wikipedia]. Useful for historical context ([How to write a family history](../guide/3-results/02-family-history.md)).

## Communities and consolidated lists

- **VGD** (vgd.ru), **j-roots**, the SVRT forum — [forums-and-communities.md](forums-and-communities.md).
- **Lost Russian Family**: a consolidated list of more than 50 free databases (repression, war, emigration to Canada, vanished villages, émigré newspapers) — lostrussianfamily.wpcomstaging.com/free-databases/ [checked]. The site is in English, the databases are mostly in Russian.
- S. Lerner's talk "Russian Language Internet Resources for Jewish Genealogy" (IAJGS 2024) — a list of Russian-language resources for Jewish genealogy ([genealogists-and-authors.md](../reading/genealogists-and-authors.md)).
