# Overview of sites by region

What this section covers: a navigation page — which genealogy sites exist for each region, from Russia and the USSR to South America and Harbin. What is already described in detail in the catalog is given here in one line with a link; only what is new is described in detail. At the end — what remains uncovered.

**How it was checked.** The links were checked on 08.10.2026 from one network (no VPN), with ordinary page requests. CAPTCHAs, browser checks and logins were not bypassed: such sites are marked "did not open". A site that did not open from one network may open from another — see [False zero](../guide/1-ai-search/09-false-zero.md), section 1. The markers ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified]) are explained in the [Glossary](../guide/0-start/06-glossary.md). Interface-language notes are in [README.md](README.md); in the tables below the languages are given only where the page itself states them. Figures (database sizes) are only those visible on the pages themselves.

## ⚠️ First: hijacked and dead addresses

As of 08.10.2026:

- **Butovo firing range** — the former site `butovo.com` is **hijacked, do not open**: instead of the memorial it redirects to unrelated sites (in one check, a gambling site). Look for the names of those shot at Butovo and "Kommunarka" through the "Open List" and Memsearch (below).
- **National Archives of Armenia** — the address `armarchives.am` is **hijacked, do not open**: it serves a bookmaker's page. The archive's real address has not been established.
- **Memorial databases** — the domains `memo.ru`, `base.memo.ru`, `lists.memo.ru`, `archive.memo.ru`, `stalin.memo.ru`, `mapofmemory.org` **do not resolve** (no addresses in DNS, as of 08.10.2026). Search-engine snapshots and old overviews still show them as working — do not believe this. Live replacements are in section 2.
- **"Book of Memory of Ukraine"** ([memory-book.ua](https://memory-book.ua/)) — the database works, but the home page has a spam block with casino links inserted into it. Follow only the menu of the database itself.

## 2. The repressed, shot, exiled, deported

**Where the Memorial databases are now.** Memorial was liquidated in Russia by a court decision (late 2021 – early 2022) [from search snippet]; its domains do not resolve (see the top of the page). What is alive in their place:

- **[Memsearch](https://memsearch.org/)** — a single search across the databases of Memorial and other memory projects (with the participation of the Czech Gulag.cz). The list of sources includes "Victims of political terror" (the former base.memo.ru), "Stalin lists", "Topography of terror", "Necropolis of terror and the Gulag", Sandarmokh, Levashovo, Krasny Bor, Perm-36 and others. Some of the sources are dead themselves, and it is unclear whether they are searchable through Memsearch's cache. Free, no login; the search is in Russian. [checked: home page, "About", "Sources"; no trial query was made]
- **[The "Open List"](https://ru.openlist.wiki/)** (Открытый список) — more than 3.3 million records; login is needed only to add to it. In detail — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md). [checked]
- **[The "Last Address"](https://www.poslednyadres.ru/)** (Последний адрес) — alive, fresh entries of July 2026. Only the people who were given plaques (Moscow, St Petersburg, other cities, Germany), not all the repressed. [checked]
- **[The "Returned Names"](https://search.hdauinp.org.ua/)** (Повернуті імена; Ukraine) — see the table below. [checked]
- **[e-memory.kz](https://e-memory.kz/)** (Kazakhstan) — see the table below. [checked]
- **[doc.arhiva.gov.md](http://doc.arhiva.gov.md/)** (Moldova) — see the table below. [checked]

**Already in the catalog and the guide:** "Immortal Barrack" (Бессмертный барак), "Returned Names" (Возвращённые имена; visz.nlr.ru), martyrology.org.ua, archive.memory.org.ua, Lithuanian databases (genocid.lt, lietuviaisibire.lt) — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md). ### Harbin, China, Shanghai

There is no single database. The basis is a talk of 2006 at the IAJGS conference (published on the JRI-Poland site): out of date, but the sources are named specifically [checked].

- **CAHJP** (Jerusalem): HIAS lists from Shanghai — 8,528 families; **YIVO** RG 243 (Shanghai 1946–1948) and RG 352 (HIAS in the Far East 1918–1955); **NARA** RG 263, roll M1750 — the archive of the Shanghai Municipal Police 1894–1949 (refugee lists, reports on Russian Jews 1933–1940).
- **Huangshan cemetery** in Harbin: 515 names — in JOWBR and on jewsofchina.org (in Cyrillic).
- **jewsofchina.org** — the association of those from China (Igud Yotsei Sin, Tel Aviv), sections by community [checked]; **KehilaLinks Harbin** and the **Sino-Judaic Institute** — open, not read.
- **BREM** — a directory of the Khabarovsk State Archive on the Bureau for Russian Emigrants in Manchuria (gakhkk.khabkrai.ru/projects/brem/) [from search snippet; the site did not connect].
- **VGD**, topics "Lists of Harbin residents and of CER (Chinese Eastern Railway) stations", "Emigration from Harbin" (forum.vgd.ru/614/) — cards of emigrants of the Shanghai police, CER directories, lists of the dead [from search snippet].

### South Africa, Australia, New Zealand

- **SA Jewish Rootsbank** — already in [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md).
- **eGGSA** (eggsa.org) — the online branch of the Genealogical Society of South Africa: church and cemetery books, passengers, voters 1925–1931, photos of gravestones; full access by paid membership; no Jewish databases [checked].
- **National Archives of Australia** — already in [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md); per search results there is a guide "Safe haven" to Jewish records. Trove, Papers Past, Archives NZ — not checked.

### Turkey: Constantinople and the evacuation of 1920

No consolidated list of those evacuated from the Crimea to Constantinople was found.
- **Hoover Institution, the Wrangel collection** (OAC, oac.cdlib.org) — 1916–1929: the evacuation from the Crimea, the resettlement of refugees in Turkey and Europe; 164 boxes, microfilm — 220 reels, partly digitized. The description has no lists by name [checked].
- **UN Archives in Geneva, the "Nansen fonds"** — files of the High Commissioner of the League of Nations for Refugees 1919–1947 [from search snippet; the site answered 429].

## What remains uncovered

**Regions**
- **Belarus:** all the sites (niab.by, nlb.by, archives.gov.by, narb.by) did not open; the description rests on search results.
- **Azerbaijan, Armenia, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan** (except the Tashkent database of evacuees): no verified fonds and no databases. The real address of the Armenian archive has not been established.
- **Georgia:** only the archive's home page opened; the "Stalin lists" did not.
- **Federal and capital archives of Russia** (GARF, RGVA, TsAMO, RGVIA, Mosgorarkhiv, the Central State Archive of St Petersburg): not one site opened.
- **Romania, Slovakia, Bulgaria, Greece, Serbia, Denmark, Finland:** browser check, no connection, or not checked.
- **Canada:** Library and Archives Canada — behind a browser check; the Ontario Jewish Archives did not open.
- **Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Paraguay, Bolivia, Panama:** only lists of Jewish organizations in old directories, no archives with a search.
- **Mexico and Chile:** only reference descriptions, no search of arrivals.
- **Japan (Kobe), India, Iran** (routes of 1939–1945), **Siberia and the Far East** as a place of exile or flight — not searched.
- **Turkey:** there is no single list of the refugees of 1920; only indirect traces (the Nansen fonds, Hoover).

**Groups of people and sources**
- **Deportations of the peoples of the USSR** and **special settlers by oblast** — there are no unified verifiable databases; only Kazakhstan, Ukraine, Moldova and Latvia were confirmed.
- **Dekulakized:** only a category in e-memory.kz and the books "Rehabilitated by History"; of the Russian regional databases "Dekulakized. Sverdlovsk oblast" was found (79,402 records, see [Orthodox and peasants](../guide/2-reference/21-orthodox-and-peasants.md), the section on the 20th century); there is no single database for the country.
- **Files in the SBU archive:** the site did not open, the procedure is known only from the 2018 article.
- **Lithuanian, Latvian, Estonian databases of the repressed:** apart from the Latvian "deported" in Raduraksti, they did not open.
- **Whether the search works** at Memsearch, "Returned Names", e-memory.kz, doc.arhiva.gov.md: the main pages and descriptions were opened, trial queries by surname were not made.
- **Not re-examined:** Chronicling America, Fold3, Trove, Papers Past, ICRC, JDC Archives (Names Index).

If you know one of these regions, let's put together a section on it together: [What is not covered in the guide](../guide/0-start/07-not-covered.md), "Let's collect together".
