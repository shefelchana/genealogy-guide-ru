Russian-language resources

What this section covers: Russian archives, libraries, databases and projects in the Russian language (apart from JewishGen and FamilySearch) — what is in them and how to work with them. Databases on the war and repression by region are in War, repression, siege. The sites in this file are in Russian unless noted; searches need Cyrillic. Don't read Russian? See For English speakers.

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified] and others) are explained in the Glossary.

⚠️ From abroad some Russian government sites do not open (in October 2026 — RGB, NEB, RNB, the Presidential Library, "Prozhito", the Rosarkhiv portal, a number of oblast archives, Gosuslugi). This is a network restriction, not a sign that the site has closed. Working from abroad: Yandex, GPIB, archive.org. See Access, CAPTCHAs, pace.

Archives

  • Yandex "Archive Search" (yandex.ru/archive) — metrical books, confession lists, revision lists of the 18th to early 20th century from the archives of Moscow and the Astrakhan, Orenburg, Irkutsk and other oblasts; a neural network recognized the handwriting. "More than 23 million archive sheets and about 6 million pages of periodicals" plus directories [checked]. The search understands word forms and pre-reform spelling. A card with techniques — archive-file-scans.md.
  • Federal archives:
    • RGADA (Russian State Archive of Ancient Acts) — fond 350 (the Landrat books and revision lists of 1707–1792) is digitized, but can be viewed only in the reading room; the reading room is free [from search snippet];
    • RGIA (Russian State Historical Archive) — the inventories are scanned, electronic orders through a personal account at rgia.su [from search snippet];
    • the single Rosarkhiv portal (online.archives.ru) — did not open on the date of checking.
  • EAIS and the electronic reading rooms of oblast archives — remote viewing of inventories and files, more often paid. Examples: in the Lipetsk archive — 60 ₽ per hour, 110 ₽ for 2 hours; in Kuzbass part of the documents is freely available after registration [checked]; search results show tariffs of "100 ₽ per day, 620 ₽ for 7 days" (archive not identified) [from search snippet]. In detail — archive-file-scans.md.
  • BLITZ (St Petersburg) — paid search in the archives of Moscow and St Petersburg; a preliminary search costs $80 (per FamilySearch).

Databases of people

  • "Open List" (Открытый список) (ru.openlist.wiki) — victims of political repression 1917–1991, 3,380,827 records, grows on the wiki principle, free [checked].
  • "Immortal Barrack" (Бессмертный барак) (bessmertnybarak.ru) — since 2015; places of birth and death, file numbers, photos, memoirs [checked: Wikipedia]; the number of records is from 2.4 to 3.35 million depending on the source [from search snippet].
  • "Returned Names" (Возвращённые имена) (visz.nlr.ru, RNB) — memorial books of the repressed, the "Leningrad Martyrology", "Siege. 1941–1944. Leningrad" [from search snippet].
  • "People's Memory" (Память народа) (pamyat-naroda.ru, Ministry of Defence) — since 2015 it has combined OBD "Memorial" and "Feat of the People" [checked: Wikipedia]. Techniques — indexed-databases.md.
  • "Road of Memory" (Дорога памяти) (1418museum.ru) — about 33–34 million participants of the war with photos; there are photos from personal files that are in no other database [from search snippet].
  • "Last Address" (Последний адрес), "Immortal Regiment" (Бессмертный полк) and others — War, repression, siege.
  • Sakharov Center martyrology (sakharov-center.ru/asfcd/martirolog) — people shot and buried in Moscow and the Moscow region in 1918–1953, from investigation files; in Russia the site is labelled as foreign-agent material [page, 2026-10-17].

Libraries and texts

  • NEB (National Electronic Library) (rusneb.ru) — a catalog of more than 41 million records, more than 5 million electronic documents; protected editions after login or in electronic reading rooms [from search snippet]. Access statuses — directories-and-books.md.
  • RNB, electronic library (National Library of Russia, nlr.ru/elibrary) — more than 650 thousand copies; the "Newspapers on the Net" (Газеты в Сети) index — more than 5,600 digitized newspapers [from search snippet].
  • RGB (Russian State Library), GPIB (Public Historical Library), the Presidential Library, "Runiverse" (Руниверс) — directories-and-books.md.
  • "Electronic Library of Historical Documents" (Электронная библиотека исторических документов) (docs.historyrussia.org) — since 2017, more than 250,000 documents [from search snippet].
  • Newspapers of the Russian Empire (CRL / East View) — press.md.
  • "Prozhito" (prozhito.org, European University at St Petersburg) — 9,009 authors, 660,000 diary entries of the 18th–20th centuries (spring 2025); search by dates, authors, place where the diary was kept [checked: Wikipedia]. Useful for historical context (How to write a family history).

Communities and consolidated lists

  • VGD (vgd.ru), j-roots, the SVRT forum — forums-and-communities.md.
  • Lost Russian Family: a consolidated list of more than 50 free databases (repression, war, emigration to Canada, vanished villages, émigré newspapers) — lostrussianfamily.wpcomstaging.com/free-databases/ [checked]. The site is in English, the databases are mostly in Russian.
  • S. Lerner's talk "Russian Language Internet Resources for Jewish Genealogy" (IAJGS 2024) — a list of Russian-language resources for Jewish genealogy (genealogists-and-authors.md).
