# DNA services

What this section covers: cards for companies and tools for genetic genealogy — what each one offers, what is free, what the limitations are. How to choose a test, work with matches under endogamy and observe ethics — see [DNA](../guide/2-reference/15-dna.md).

Information as of September–October 2026; prices and rules change.

**The main rule for Ashkenazim:** because of endogamy, look at the **longest shared segment**, not the sum of centimorgans (cM). A practical guide: under 15 cM — discard, from 20 cM — look at the tree, from 30 cM — write to the person; a shared 200–250 cM and above is almost certainly a traceable relationship. In detail and with other thresholds — in [DNA](../guide/2-reference/15-dna.md).

### 23andMe
- **What is there:** DNA Relatives (a list of genetic relatives), Ancestry Composition (an estimate of origin, with subregions, for example "West Central Ukrainian Jews"), Relatives in Common.
- **Useful:** filters by surname and by ancestors' place of birth are free; the haplogroup filter is only in paid Premium. The **export of the relatives list** has a column for the Y-haplogroup (filled in regardless of whether the person accepted the invitation) and country of residence.
- **Does not accept** other companies' raw data.
- Bankruptcy (Chapter 11) on 23.03.2025; on 14.07.2025 the assets were bought for $305 million by the nonprofit TTAM Research Institute (founder — Anne Wojcicki), and the service continues to operate [page: Wikipedia "23andMe", 2026-10-07].

### FamilyTreeDNA (familytreedna.com)
- **What is there:** upload (transfer) of raw data from other companies — matches are free; the $19 unlock of advanced features was abolished from 18.08.2026 — the chromosome browser and myOrigins are now through a paid upgrade (per dna-explained.com — $29; not cross-checked on help.familytreedna.com, 403) [page: dna-explained.com, 2026-10-07]; what is uploaded cannot be downloaded back; **Y-DNA** (Big Y-700 — $449 on 22.09.2026); mtDNA.
- **Limitation:** according to the official help, it **does not ship test kits to Russia, Belarus and Ukraine**. Fallback options for a Y-test: YSEQ (Berlin, whole genome about $399), YFull from a ready BAM file, forwarding through a third country.
- **Why a Y-test:** the only way to answer whether this is the same paternal line where the documents break off; it finds relatives with other surnames. Test the oldest man in the direct male line.
- Sorts matches by the longest segment.

### MyHeritage DNA
- **Uploading other people's data is closed:** since May 2025 MyHeritage does not accept raw DNA files from other companies — matches there exist only for those who took a MyHeritage test (MyHeritage help "Can I upload an autosomal DNA file to MyHeritage?"; analysis — The DNA Geek, "The End of an Era: Uploads at MyHeritage").
- **Tests are unavailable in some countries** (an official restriction). Check before ordering.
- The service removed the built-in export of matches to a table; work with screenshots and the "Has Theory of Family Relativity" filter. Details of "Record Matches" require a paid subscription.
- Sorts matches by the longest segment.

### GEDmatch (gedmatch.com)
- **What is there:** comparison of data from different companies in one place (upload of a raw file).
- **Free:** One-to-Many Free Limited — the first 50 matches, segments from 7 cM; one-to-one comparison. Tier 1 — $15 a month or $150 a year for new subscribers from 01.09.2026; earlier subscribers keep $10 / $100 (GEDmatch mailing, from search results 2026-10-07).
- **Experience:** matches that fit by a long segment often **have no tree attached** — there is nothing to compare with. Attach a link to your own tree to your profile.
- **Login quirk:** the page sometimes hangs on the two-factor-protection reminder screen ("Something went wrong") — going directly to `app.gedmatch.com/select.php` helps.
- Police access — only with the user's explicit consent (per the GEDmatch blog).

### Living DNA
- Free upload of other people's data, basic matches.

### AncestryDNA
- The test is not sold everywhere (per the official help on 25.09.2026 — in 119 countries), and it **does not accept other people's raw data**. The longest segment cannot be sorted in the interface (segments are visible only in an export via DNAGedcom); it is shown before general-population segments are subtracted.

### Genotek (genotek.ru) (Russian-language)
- A Russian service. For free you see 2–3 "teaser" names in notifications; the full list of relatives and the family tree are a paid product (990–2995 ₽). I could not assess its usefulness compared with the others.

## Tools

- **DNA Painter** (dnapainter.com) — mapping segments onto known ancestors (raise the minimum threshold to 10–15 cM); the Shared cM Project table (⚠️ does not take endogamy into account).
- **DNAGedcom** — export of segments, including from Ancestry.
- **YFull**, **YSEQ** — analysis and tests for Y-DNA.
- An overview of where to upload raw data: ancestrify.io/blog/where-to-upload-raw-dna.
