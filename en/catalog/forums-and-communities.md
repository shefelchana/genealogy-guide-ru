# Forums and communities: practical techniques

What this section covers: where to ask for advice, find "co-researchers" and get help with translation, the technical quirks of these sites, and how to join joint projects as a volunteer. The full list of groups and communities (including on Facebook) is in [groups-and-communities.md](../reading/groups-and-communities.md). Markers and interface-language notes are in [README.md](README.md). Don't read Russian? See [For English speakers](../guide/0-start/08-for-english-speakers.md).

---

### j-roots.info — the "Jewish Roots" forum (Еврейские корни; forum.j-roots.info) (in Russian)
- **What is there:** a Russian-language forum of Jewish genealogy (since 2009). Topics on shtetls, on the fonds of oblast archives, a general surname index. Posts carry direct links to digitized files and page-by-page indexes; hidden blocks "► SHOW" (ПОКАЗАТЬ) open with a click. The site's **databases** ("Archive references" (Архивные ссылки) — an index of surnames from file titles; the "Stone Archive" (Каменный архив) — cemeteries) are available only under a forum login.
- **Access:** topics can be read without login; forum search and the databases — only for registered users (free).
- **Quirks (September–October 2026):**
  - links from search results with the parameters `hilit` / `sid` give error 403 — open `viewtopic.php?t=<number>` directly;
  - the new interface of the databases hung on "searching" — the classic form `searchdb_form.php?old` helped;
  - through a foreign VPN the site was silent; in early October the site's certificate was expiring — be careful with security exceptions.
- **Experience:** a link to a digitized list of victims of the 1919 pogrom and hints about fonds.

### The VGD forum — "All-Russian Genealogical Tree" (Всероссийское генеалогическое древо; forum.vgd.ru, vgd.ru) (in Russian)
- A forum with surname and regional topics, a section on Jewish genealogy, a directory of settlements, articles on paleography, a section "DNA genealogy. Beginners' questions". Registration and a tree are free; there are premium features and freelancers' services [checked]. A useful article for beginners: docs.vgd.ru/kak-najti-predkov.

### The SVRT forum, section "Jewish genealogy" (forum.svrt.ru) (in Russian)
- Can be read without login, posting after registration; opening surname topics is forbidden. **Activity is weak:** 25 topics, the latest visible messages are from 2025 and earlier [checked, 08.10.2026]. It does not work as a replacement for j-roots.

### Telegram: the Familio chats (chat.familio.org)
- Free chats by country and topic: "Jewish genealogy" (about 1,500 members), Ukraine, Belarus, Moldova, Poland, Lithuania, Latvia, "Reading old handwriting" (about 9,300), indexing, DNA. Sizes and rules — in [groups-and-communities.md](../reading/groups-and-communities.md) [checked].
- **Technically:** the chats have no public feed — without logging in only the number of members is visible; the content was not checked. In the "Jewish genealogy" chat it is forbidden to recommend paid researchers.

### JewishGen Family Finder (JGFF) (English interface)
- You enter your surnames and shtetls and see who is looking for the same; free JewishGen registration.
- Every researcher has a contact button — the message goes through the site, the address is not revealed.
- **Technically:** the "Send my message" button works only from a real mouse click; check the result by the text "Your message was forwarded", otherwise the message may remain in the form.
- **Advice:** enter **all** your places and surnames in your profile — JGFF itself sends notifications of matches.
- **Experience:** of four messages to researchers of the same surnames and places one grew into a correspondence with new information. It is better to write when you have something to offer in return. How to write — [Requests and letters](../guide/2-reference/20-requests-letters.md).

### ViewMate (JewishGen) (English interface)
- You post a scan or photo — volunteers help free of charge to translate or read it (Yiddish, Hebrew, Russian). The image stays up for 7 days, then goes to the archive together with the answers. Professionals with paid services are forbidden to use it [from search snippet: ViewMate FAQ]. JewishGen registration is needed.

### JewishGen Discussion Group (groups.jewishgen.org/g/main) (English interface)
- A mailing list with an archive since 1993 (more than 100,000 messages) [checked: jewishgen.org]; a search of the archive often finds the answer to your question. Free registration; joining and the first posts are approved by moderators.
- **Technically:** the group page without login answers 402 or requires JavaScript — the freshness of discussions cannot be checked with a script.

### Regional divisions of JewishGen
- Ukraine Research Division, Bessarabia SIG, Belarus SIG, LitvakSIG, ROM-SIG and others: databases, mailing lists, shtetl "Town Leaders". See [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md), [europe-by-country.md](europe-by-country.md).

## Joint projects and hubs: how to join as a volunteer

What this section covers: where you can not only ask but help — index, translate, run a shtetl page, transcribe a cemetery. A volunteer often gets the data of his or her district before everyone else. Checked 08.10.2026.

| Project | What they do | How to join | What you need to be able to do | Marker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **KehilaLinks** (kehilalinks.jewishgen.org) | shtetl pages: history, photos, lists | check the catalog: if the page exists — write to the owner; if not — contact the coordinators and choose a shtetl; JewishGen trains | HTML is not required: Ukraine RD has a page builder | [from search snippet]; [checked: PDF for Town Leaders] |
| **Town Leaders** (Ukraine RD, about 800 towns) | a "town leader" runs the shtetl's page and connects researchers through JGFF | the list of towns without a leader is on the Ukraine RD site (GEO_townlist.asp); the project director's contact is in the PDF "Getting started as a Town Leader" (August 2024) | willingness to write to people | [checked] |
| **Ukraine RD**, volunteers | translators, transcribers, project leaders | two short questionnaires in the "About Us" section; you can also donate for the transcription of chosen towns | reading Cyrillic | [checked] |
| **Bessarabia SIG** | revision lists, metrical books, court records, deportations, cemeteries (96 cemeteries by the end of 2025) | write to the coordinator (E. Kogan); translators from Romanian and Russian are needed | Russian cursive, Excel | [checked: 2026 news]; terms [from search snippet] |
| **Belarus SIG** | translations from Yiddish, Hebrew, old cursive; coordinators of towns and districts | through the Help Desk on the site | Yiddish, Hebrew, Russian | [from search snippet] |
| **LitvakSIG** | 14 district groups; more than 650 thousand of about 800 thousand metrical records translated | the "Volunteer Opportunities" page; members of district groups get the tables at least 18 months before public search | Russian, Polish, Yiddish | [from search snippet]; [checked: FAQ] |
| **Gesher Galicia** | metrical books, Holocaust, maps, NKVD files, voter lists | membership from $50 a year; contact on the site | Polish, German, Ukrainian | [checked] |
| **JRI-Poland** | indexing of Polish, Russian, German metrical books; checking of indexes; Town Leader | write to the address on the page jri-poland.org/get-involved/; knowledge of languages is not required | any | [checked] |
| **JOWBR / IAJGS Cemetery Project** | "adopt a cemetery": transfer a whole cemetery or a hometown-society plot into a table | the volunteer form on JewishGen: tables from photos, translation of inscriptions from Hebrew and Cyrillic. Data of a single family is not accepted | Hebrew, Cyrillic | [from search snippet] |
| **"Jewish Roots": digitization** (forum.j-roots.info) | indexes on Odesa for the Odesa archive (last message 27.08.2026), lists of gymnasium pupils of Ukraine (28.07.2026), translation from gravestones; instructions and table templates | registration on the forum and a reply in the right topic; the menu item "For volunteers" (Волонтёрам) | cursive, Excel | [checked] |
| **Familio: "Indexing and transcription"** (Индексация и расшифровка; t.me/familio_indexing, about 3,300 members) and Familio Index (index.familio.org) | joint transcription and an index of sources | join the chat by the link | Russian cursive | [checked: the chats page]; contents [unverified] |

If there is no suitable project, start your own hub on a topic the guide lacks: [What is not covered in the guide](../guide/0-start/07-not-covered.md), "Let's collect together".
