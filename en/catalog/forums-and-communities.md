# Forums and communities: practical techniques

What this section covers: where to ask for advice, find "co-researchers" and get help with translation — and the technical quirks of these sites. The full list of groups and communities (including on Facebook) is in [../reading/groups-and-communities.md](../reading/groups-and-communities.md). Markers are in [README.md](README.md).

### j-roots.info — the "Evreiskie korni" (Jewish Roots) forum (forum.j-roots.info) (Russian-language)
- **What is there:** a Russian-language Jewish genealogy forum (since 2009). Topics on shtetls, on the fonds of oblast archives, a general surname index. The posts contain direct links to digitizations and page-by-page indexes; hidden blocks "► ПОКАЗАТЬ" (SHOW) open with a click. **The site's databases** ("Архивные ссылки" [Archival references] — an index of surnames from the titles of files; "Каменный архив" [Stone archive] — cemeteries) are available only with a forum login.
- **Access:** topics can be read without logging in; the forum search and the databases are for registered users only (free).
- **Quirks (September–October 2026):**
  - links from search results with the parameters `hilit` / `sid` give a 403 error — open `viewtopic.php?t=<number>` directly;
  - the new interface of the databases hung on "search in progress" — the classic form `searchdb_form.php?old` helped;
  - through a foreign VPN the site was silent; in early October the site's certificate was expiring — be careful with security exceptions.
- **Experience:** a link to a digitized list of victims of the 1919 pogrom and tips on fonds.

### VGD forum — "Vserossiiskoe genealogicheskoe drevo" (All-Russian Genealogical Tree) (forum.vgd.ru, vgd.ru) (Russian-language)
- A forum with surname and regional topics, a section on Jewish genealogy, a directory of localities, articles on paleography, a section "DNK-genealogiya. Voprosy novichkov" (DNA genealogy. Beginners' questions). Registration and a tree are free; there is a premium tier and services of freelancers [checked]. A useful article for beginners: docs.vgd.ru/kak-najti-predkov.

### JewishGen Family Finder (JGFF)
- You enter your surnames and shtetls and see who is looking for the same; free registration at JewishGen.
- Every researcher has a contact button — the letter goes through the site, the address is not revealed.
- **Technically:** the "Send my message" button works only from a real mouse click; check the result by the text "Your message was forwarded", otherwise the letter may stay in the form.
- **Advice:** put **all** your places and surnames in your profile — JGFF itself sends notifications about matches.
- **Experience:** of four letters to researchers of the same surnames and places, one grew into a correspondence with new information. It is better to write when you have something to offer in return. How to write — [Requests and letters](../guide/2-reference/20-requests-letters.md).

### ViewMate (JewishGen)
- You post a scan or photo — volunteers help translate or read it for free (Yiddish, Hebrew, Russian). The image stays up for 7 days, then goes to the archive with the answers. Professionals with paid services are forbidden to use it [from search results: ViewMate FAQ]. JewishGen registration is required.

### JewishGen Discussion Group (groups.jewishgen.org/g/main)
- A mailing list with an archive since 1998; searching the archive often finds the answer to your question. Free registration.

### Regional divisions of JewishGen
- Ukraine Research Division, Bessarabia SIG, Belarus SIG, LitvakSIG, ROM-SIG and others: databases, mailing lists, "Town Leaders" of shtetls. See [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md), [europe-by-country.md](europe-by-country.md).
