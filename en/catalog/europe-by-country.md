# Europe by country: resources for people from the Russian Empire

What this section covers: databases and archives of European countries where documents on ancestors from the Russian Empire are kept — from Poland and the Baltics to Germany, France and Great Britain. A short map of archives is in [Archives by country](../guide/2-reference/07-archives-by-country.md).

Markers: **[checked]** — read on the page (October 2026); **[from search snippet]** — from search results, to be verified; "not verified on the page".

---

## Poland
- **JRI-Poland** — card in [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **Geneteka** (geneteka.genealodzy.pl, Polish Genealogical Society) — 68.2 million records, 6,281 parishes, 16,093 books; 16 Polish regions, as well as Ukraine, Belarus, Lithuania; free [checked]. Whether records of non-Christian communities are indexed in each region — not verified on the page.
- **szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl** — over 55 million scans from Polish state archives. Search by the **name of the locality**, ticking "Vital records and civil registers"; documents of the last 80–100 years are in the civil registry offices (ZAGS-type offices). Languages: Russian or German for the former partitions, Latin in church books [from search snippet]. ⚠️ The site blocked some foreign addresses entirely.
- **PRADZIAD** — shows where the books of a parish or civil registry office are kept [from search snippet].
- **AGAD** (Warsaw) — books of the former Lwów, Tarnopol, Stanisławów and Volhynia voivodeships; a series of Jewish books from beyond the Bug 1789–1943 — freely available through szukajwarchiwach [from search snippet]; about 3,000 Jewish metrical books of Galicia 1877–1911 online [per FamilySearch].
- **Gesher Galicia** — the All Galicia Database (over half a million records 1786–1945) has been closed behind membership since September 2026 (from the research experience; no announcement of this was found on geshergalicia.org — **not verified on the page**, 2026-10-07).

## Lithuania
- **LitvakSIG** (litvaksig.org) — the All Lithuania Database, over 2.8 million records, updated quarterly, free. Those who contribute money to the district research groups get the data earlier [checked].
- **epaveldas.lt** — per search results, 2,312 books from 91 Catholic parishes, 1599–1940, scans from the Lithuanian State Historical Archives (LVIA) [from search snippet]. LVIA Jewish metrical books — from the mid-19th century to 1940 [from search snippet]; whether they are on epaveldas — not verified.
- **LVIA / archyvai.lt** — the genealogical search page did not open (403).

## Latvia
- **Raduraksti** (raduraksti.arhivi.lv) — church books of all confessions, revisions of Vidzeme and Kurzeme, Riga house books, the 1897 census, students of the University of Latvia 1919–1944 [checked]. Jewish metrical books — in the section "Church books → Mosaic faith → rabbis"; the abbreviations Dz/L/M/Šķ — births, marriages, deaths, divorces. The 1897 census — by towns and precincts, manually by archival references. Free registration is required; the interface is in Latvian only [page: Latvia SIG].

## Estonia
- **Rahvusarhiiv** (National Archives): **Saaga** (digitized documents) and **AIS** (search across all Estonian archives, keyword "Juudi"). Jewish metrical books of the communities of Tallinn, Tartu, Võru, Valga, Rakvere, Narva, Viljandi. Registration is free [page: Latvia SIG]. At ra.ee/apps/andmed — a register of revisions, Album Academicum 1918–1944 and others [checked].
- **The Yuryev (Tartu) University of the imperial period** — [foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md).

## Belarus
- **NIAB** (National Historical Archives of Belarus, Minsk) — the main source for the 16th – early 20th century: metrical books, revisions, synagogue records; 29 fonds of Jewish communities of Minsk and Mogilev guberniyas. Search — paid requests; NIAB in Grodno accepts requests through its site [from search snippet]. An inventory of NIAB fonds for Jewish genealogy was compiled with funds from Belarus SIG [from search snippet]. The sites did not open.
- **Radzima.org** — a place directory; **JewishGen Belarus Database**; a translation of the name index of the "Rossiiskaya evreiskaya entsiklopediya" (Russian Jewish Encyclopedia) on the Belarus SIG site — see [../reading/dictionaries-and-encyclopedias.md](../reading/dictionaries-and-encyclopedias.md).

## Ukraine
- Scans, catalogs and inventories — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md). The Soviet period and the war — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md).

## Moldova (Bessarabia)
- **NARM / ANRM** (National Archives of the Republic of Moldova, Chisinau) — the metrical books of the rabbinates of Chisinau, Bălți and Bender have survived well [from search snippet]. Metrical books of Chisinau, Cahul (1864–1878), Bolgrad (1871), revisions of Izmail 1824–1854, lists of 1897 and 1905 — on FamilySearch films ([archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md)).
- The reading room is free, but, per search results, an application with a letter of recommendation is needed; a pass is issued in 3 days [from search snippet].
- **The Romanian period of southern Bessarabia (1856–1878):** someone born then in Izmail, Kiliya, Bolgrad, Cahul is recorded in Romanian registration. There are no Romanian records of Izmail for 1865–1878 on FamilySearch; the collection "Romania, Civil Registration 1865–1903" is very small. The guide to the Izmail archive (1966) confirms: there are no materials for 1856–1878.
- **Bessarabia SIG**, Bessarabia Geographical Dictionary, bessarabia.ru — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).

## Romania
- **ROM-SIG** (JewishGen Romania Research Division) — transcriptions and a database for Romania. **It does not cover Bessarabia and Transylvania:** those are handled by Bessarabia SIG and the Hungarian Research Division [checked].
- **National Archives of Romania (ANR)** — the pages did not open, not verified.
- **Pinkas HaKehillot, the "Romania" volume** (Yad Vashem) includes the communities of Bessarabia; an English translation is on JewishGen [from search results].

## Germany
- **Arolsen Archives** — [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **Bundesarchiv Gedenkbuch** (apps.bundesarchiv.de/gedenkbuch) — victims of the persecution of Jews in Germany 1933–1945. Search by place of birth, residence and deportation, by dates, with wildcards * and ?; export to a table [checked].
- **Hamburg and Bremen departure lists** — [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md).
- **Soviet prisoners of war** — the Dokumentationsstelle Dresden database ([War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md)).

## Austria
- **GenTeam** (genteam.at) — over 23 million records, 50 databases: Vienna (including the Jewish metrical books of the IKG community), Jewish gravestones, newspaper obituaries. Free after registration [checked].
- **University of Vienna, PHAIDRA** — student forms with the father's name, occupation and place of residence ([foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md)).

## Belgium
- Police files on foreigners: FelixArchief (Antwerp) and the State Archives in Brussels — [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md); search experience — [foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md).

## France
- **Archives nationales** — about 800,000 freely accessible naturalization files (subseries BB/11, 1789–1930). The decrees BB/34 for 1899–1912 and 1928 are indexed — about 400,000 names in the virtual reading room [checked].
- **Mémorial de la Shoah** — an archive of over 80 million documents; the online catalog is cut down, without data on private life; for information about a person — through the form "Demande de renseignements sur une personne" [checked].

## Great Britain
- **National Archives (Kew)**: naturalization files HO 1 (1801–1871), HO 144 (1872–1934), certificates HO 334 (1870–1986). The certificate gives place of birth, **names of parents**, spouse, children. Mostly not free online; in the Discovery catalog search "surname nat*" (1949–1981); 1870–1916 — on Ancestry [checked].
