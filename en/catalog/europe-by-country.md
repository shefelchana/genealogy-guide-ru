# Europe by country: resources for people from the Russian Empire

What this section covers: databases and archives of European countries where documents about ancestors from the Russian Empire are kept — from Poland and the Baltics to Germany, France and Great Britain. A short map of archives is in [Archives by country](../guide/2-reference/07-archives-by-country.md).

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified] and others) are explained in the [Glossary](../guide/0-start/06-glossary.md). Interface-language notes are explained in [README.md](README.md).

---

## Poland
- **JRI-Poland** — card in [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **Geneteka** (geneteka.genealodzy.pl, Polish Genealogical Society) — 68.2 million records, 6,281 parishes, 16,093 books; 16 Polish regions, and also Ukraine, Belarus, Lithuania; free [checked]. Whether records of non-Christian communities are indexed in each region — not verified on the page.
- **szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl** — more than 55 million scans of Polish state archives. Search by the **name of the locality**, ticking "Vital records and civil registers"; documents of the last 80–100 years are in the civil registry offices (ZAGS). Languages: Russian or German for the former partitions, Latin in church books [from search snippet]. ⚠️ The site blocked some foreign addresses entirely.
- **PRADZIAD** — shows where the books of a parish or civil registry are kept [from search snippet].
- **AGAD** (Warsaw) — books of the former Lwów, Tarnopol, Stanisławów and Volhynia voivodeships; the series of Jewish books from beyond the Bug 1789–1943 is freely accessible through szukajwarchiwach [from search snippet]; about 3,000 Jewish metrical books of Galicia 1877–1911 online [per FamilySearch].
- **Gesher Galicia** — the All Galicia Database (over half a million records 1786–1945) has been behind a membership wall since September 2026 (from the research project's experience; no announcement was found on geshergalicia.org — **not verified on the page**, 2026-10-07).

## Lithuania
- **LitvakSIG** (litvaksig.org) — the All Lithuania Database, over 2.8 million records, updated quarterly, free. Those who contribute money to the district research groups get the data earlier [checked].
- **epaveldas.lt** — per search results, 2,312 books from 91 Catholic parishes, 1599–1940, scans from the Lithuanian State Historical Archives (LVIA) [from search snippet]. Jewish metrical books of LVIA — from the mid-19th century to 1940 [from search snippet]; whether they are on epaveldas — not verified.
- **LVIA / archyvai.lt** — the genealogical search page did not open on the date of checking.

## Latvia
- **Raduraksti** (raduraksti.arhivi.lv) (Latvian only) — church books of all confessions, revisions of Vidzeme and Kurzeme, house books of Riga, the 1897 census, students of the University of Latvia 1919–1944 [checked]. New collections (on the home page on 08.10.2026): **passports of residents of Latvia 1918–1940** ("Latvijas iedzīvotāju pasu kolekcija") and **the deported 1941–1953** ("Deportētie Latvijas iedzīvotāji" — a list of those deported by the Soviet regime) [checked]. Jewish metrical books are in the section "Church books → Mosaic faith → rabbis"; the abbreviations Dz/L/M/Šķ stand for births, marriages, deaths, divorces. The 1897 census is by towns and sections, by hand through archival references. Free registration is needed; the interface is only in Latvian [checked: Latvia SIG].

## Estonia
- **Rahvusarhiiv** (National Archives): **AIS** (ais.ra.ee — search across all Estonian archives, keyword "Juudi"). Jewish metrical books of the communities of Tallinn, Tartu, Võru, Valga, Rakvere, Narva, Viljandi. Registration is free [checked: Latvia SIG]. At ra.ee/apps/andmed — the register of revisions, Album Academicum 1918–1944 and others [checked].
  - ⚠️ **Saaga:** the old address saaga.ra.ee did not connect on 08.10.2026 (the name is not found in DNS), and saaga.ee is an unrelated site, not the archive. According to AIS's own description, digitized files are read directly in it; the search has the filters "Ainult digitaalsed allikad" (digital only) and "Virtuaalne uurimissaal" (virtual reading room) [checked: ais.ra.ee]. Whether the Saaga viewer has been moved into AIS completely is not verified [unverified].
- **The Imperial-period University of Yuryev (Tartu)** — [foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md).

## Belarus
- **NIAB** (National Historical Archives of Belarus, Minsk) — the main source for the 16th to early 20th century: metrical books, revisions, synagogue records; 29 fonds of Jewish communities of Minsk and Mogilev guberniyas. Search is by paid requests; NIAB in Grodno accepts requests through its website [from search snippet]. An inventory of NIAB fonds for Jewish genealogy was compiled with funds from Belarus SIG [from search snippet]. The sites did not open.
- **Radzima.org** — a place directory; **JewishGen Belarus Database**; a translation of the name index of the "Russian Jewish Encyclopedia" on the Belarus SIG site — see [dictionaries-and-encyclopedias.md](../reading/dictionaries-and-encyclopedias.md).

## Ukraine
- Scans, catalogs and inventories — [archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md). Soviet period and the war — [War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md).

## Moldova (Bessarabia)
- **ANRM** (National Archives of the Republic of Moldova, Chișinău) — the metrical books of the rabbinates of Chișinău, Bălți and Bender have survived well [from search snippet]. Metrical books of Chișinău, Cahul (1864–1878), Bolgrad (1871), revisions of Izmail 1824–1854, the lists of 1897 and 1905 — on FamilySearch films ([archive-file-scans.md](archive-file-scans.md)).
- The reading room is free, but, per search results, you need an application with a letter of recommendation; the pass is issued within 3 days [from search snippet].
- **The Romanian period of southern Bessarabia (1856–1878):** a person born then in Izmail, Kiliya, Bolgrad or Cahul was recorded in Romanian registration. There are no Romanian records of Izmail for 1865–1878 on FamilySearch; the collection "Romania, Civil Registration 1865–1903" is very small. The guide to the Izmail archive (1966) confirms: there are no materials for 1856–1878.
- **Bessarabia SIG**, Bessarabia Geographical Dictionary, bessarabia.ru — [directories-and-books.md](directories-and-books.md).

## Romania
- **ROM-SIG** (JewishGen Romania Research Division) — transcriptions and a database for Romania. It **does not cover Bessarabia and Transylvania:** those are handled by Bessarabia SIG and the Hungarian Research Division [checked].
- **National Archives of Romania (ANR)** — the pages did not open, not verified.
- **Pinkas HaKehillot, volume "Romania"** (Yad Vashem) includes the communities of Bessarabia; an English translation is on JewishGen [from search snippet].

## Germany
- **Arolsen Archives** — [indexed-databases.md](indexed-databases.md).
- **Bundesarchiv Gedenkbuch** (apps.bundesarchiv.de/gedenkbuch) — victims of the persecution of Jews in Germany 1933–1945. Search by place of birth, residence and deportation, by dates, with wildcards * and ?; export to a table [checked].
- **Hamburg and Bremen departure lists** — [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md).
- **Soviet prisoners of war** — the database of the Dokumentationsstelle Dresden ([War, repression, siege](../guide/2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md)).

## Austria
- **GenTeam** (genteam.at) — more than 23 million records, 50 databases: Vienna (including the Jewish metrical books of the IKG community), Jewish gravestones, obituaries from newspapers. Free after registration [checked].
- **University of Vienna, PHAIDRA** — students' forms with the name, occupation and place of residence of the father ([foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md)).

## Belgium
- Police files on foreigners: FelixArchief (Antwerp) and the State Archives in Brussels — [usa-and-emigration.md](usa-and-emigration.md); search experience — [foreign-and-university-archives.md](foreign-and-university-archives.md).

## France
- **Archives nationales** — about 800,000 freely accessible naturalization files (subseries BB/11, 1789–1930). The decrees BB/34 for 1899–1912 and 1928 are indexed — about 400,000 names in the virtual reading room [checked].
- **Mémorial de la Shoah** — an archive of more than 80 million documents; the online catalog is cut down, with no data on private life; for a person — through the form "Demande de renseignements sur une personne" [checked].

## Great Britain
- **National Archives (Kew)**: naturalization files HO 1 (1801–1871), HO 144 (1872–1934), certificates HO 334 (1870–1986). A certificate has the place of birth, the **names of the parents**, spouse, children. Mostly not free online; in the Discovery catalog search "surname nat*" (1949–1981); 1870–1916 — on Ancestry [checked].
