# Tracing Your Ancestors: A Practical Guide
### Russian Empire, USSR, Jewish families, emigration — a search route, sources, and working with AI

> Version 0.4, October 2026. The guide keeps growing; websites change — check the links.

A guide for people who look for their relatives and information about them in documents: metrical books, revision lists, censuses, newspapers, military and emigration databases, cemeteries and DNA. The territory is the former Russian Empire and the USSR (Ukraine, Moldova/Bessarabia, Belarus, Russia, Poland, Lithuania) and emigration to the USA and Europe.

**An honest word about focus.** The guide grew out of the search for Jewish families of Podolia, the Kyiv region and Bessarabia, and that is what it covers in the most depth. The search route, the checking of finds and the keeping of records work for any family. But Orthodox and peasant lines, the early revisions I–IV (1719–1782), confession lists, consistories and non-Jewish cemeteries are covered only a little. What there is can be found in [Records of the Russian Empire and the USSR](guide/2-reference/01-records-empire-ussr.md), in the section on [Russian regional archives and the unified archival information system (ЕАИС, EAIS)](catalog/archive-file-scans.md) and in [Russian-language resources](catalog/russian-language-resources.md). General resources are in the [catalog of sites](catalog/README.md); a community for non-Jewish genealogy is the VGD forum (Всероссийское генеалогическое древо; see [Groups and communities](reading/groups-and-communities.md)).

The guide is written for two readers at once: for a **human** and for their **AI assistant** (Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and others). The material is drawn from open guides and communities (links to authors and sources are in each section and in [reading/](reading/README.md)) and from the practical experience of a real family research project carried out with the help of AI agents in **Claude Code**. But you do not need to work with agents: you can start with a single check in a browser with an AI plugin — see [Three levels](guide/0-start/00-three-levels.md).

**The focus in brief:** the emphasis is on Jewish genealogy; what is covered only a little is listed in [What this guide does not cover](guide/0-start/07-not-covered.md).

## Where to start

- **I'm just beginning** — [Quick start](guide/0-start/01-quick-start.md): one page, the first hour of work. Unfamiliar words are in the [Glossary](guide/0-start/06-glossary.md).
- **I'm stuck** — [Scenarios: what to do if…](guide/1-ai-search/07-scenarios.md).
- **I'm an AI agent** — start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) (instructions for the agent) and [llms.txt](llms.txt) (a map of the guide). First ask the person about their goal and what is already known about the family.

The whole guide follows one route: **question → place → source → index → scan → verification → record**. Details are in the [Search route](guide/1-ai-search/01-search-route.md).

## How to read

Read the beginning in order: the sections are arranged in the order of the work. **The Search route, Checking results and Scenarios** sit in Part 1, but they are for everyone, not only for those who work with AI: they are the general order of a search and the way out of dead ends. Open the Reference (Part 2) as needed: every section has a "How to ask AI" box. The marks in square brackets (**[checked]**, **[from search snippet]**, **[unverified]**) show how well a piece of information has been checked; what they mean is explained at the end of the [Glossary](guide/0-start/06-glossary.md).

## Skills for Claude — how to install

The [skills/](../skills/README.md) folder contains 26 skills: from the first conversation ("I want to find my ancestors") to reading scans, revisions, pogrom databases, cemeteries, DNA and writing the family history. Claude loads the right skill itself when the task fits. Details — [Skills for Claude](guide/1-ai-search/06-skills.md).

The skills are written in Russian, but Claude reads them and works fine with prompts in English.

**Claude Code — as a plugin** (skills and agents at once):
```
/plugin marketplace add shefelchana/genealogy-guide-ru
/plugin install genealogy-guide-ru@genealogy-guide-ru
```

**Claude Code — manually:** copy the folders from `skills/` into `~/.claude/skills/`, and the files from `agents/` into `~/.claude/agents/`.

**Claude.ai and the Claude app:** on paid plans the plugin can be added there too: Customize → Plugins → Add → Add marketplace, the repository address `shefelchana/genealogy-guide-ru`. A single skill can also be uploaded without the plugin: zip the whole skill folder (a ZIP containing a folder with `SKILL.md`) and add it under Customize → Skills; skills require code execution to be enabled. Scripts from the `scripts/` folders may not run in the web version (for example, without network access) [unverified].

More details and limitations — [skills/README.md](../skills/README.md).
