# What this guide does not cover

What this section covers: what the guide rests on, where it is strong, and where there is little information or none at all. That does not mean nothing can be found there — it means you will find little here. Such topics are best built together (see the end).

---

## What it is built on

The guide grew out of the search for **Jewish families of the Russian Empire** — Podolia, Kyiv guberniya, Bessarabia — and their descendants in the USSR, Israel and the USA. Hence the emphasis:
- Jewish genealogy: the metrical books of crown rabbis, revisions of meshchane, the Pale of Settlement, the Holocaust, emigration;
- Ukraine, Russia, Moldova, in part Poland, Belarus and the Baltics;
- the USA as the main direction of emigration.

**Much of it suits other searches too**: the revision ladder, the standard of proof, working with scans, "not found" with coverage, the process with AI agents, DNA, keeping files. But the techniques and sources were chosen for the Jewish case, and something for your case has surely been missed.

## What is covered little or not at all

**Other faiths and estates of the Russian Empire**
- Orthodox metrical books and confession lists, consistory fonds;
- Catholics, Lutherans, Reformed, Old Believers, Muslims, Karaites;
- peasants: serfs, state peasants, revisions by landowners' estates, volost boards;
- nobles, clergy, Cossacks, military estates — each has its own archives.

A beginning has been made: [Orthodox, peasants and other non-Jewish lines](../2-reference/21-orthodox-and-peasants.md) — metrical books and confession lists, peasant revisions, Catholics, Lutherans, Old Believers. Muslims, Karaites and the nobility are not yet covered.

**Other Jewish communities**
- Sephardim and those from the Ottoman Empire, North Africa, the Middle East;
- Mountain, Bukharan and Georgian Jews; Krymchaks;
- Central Asia and the Caucasus as a whole.

**Soviet repression** — covered briefly ([War, repression, the siege](../2-reference/12-war-repression-siege.md)); the first links by region, including working replacements for the Memorial databases, are in the [Regional overview of sites](../../catalog/regional-overview.md), section 2
- the shot and their investigation files;
- the exiled and special settlers, the dekulakised;
- deportations of peoples (Germans, Crimean Tatars, peoples of the Caucasus, residents of the Baltics and Western Ukraine in the 1940s–1950s);
- the Gulag, "family members of a traitor to the Motherland", children's homes.

**Directions of emigration and flight** — apart from the USA and Israel, almost nothing; the first links are in the [Regional overview of sites](../../catalog/regional-overview.md), sections 4–5
- South America: Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Chile;
- Canada, Great Britain, France, Germany as a country of transit;
- Harbin, Shanghai and China; Turkey and the evacuation of 1920;
- South Africa, Australia, New Zealand; Mexico, Cuba.

**Other topics**
- adoption, foster families, foundlings, children's homes;
- Y-DNA and mtDNA searching beyond the basics; genetic genealogy for unknown parentage;
- Western Europe as the main region of the search (rather than transit).

## Let's build it together

If you know one of these topics — sources, databases, techniques, traps — let's make a shared section or a separate "hub" on it: a collection of resources tested in practice, with notes on what works and what does not. One person cannot cover everything, but together it can be done.

How to join:
- write on Telegram **[@shefel](https://t.me/shefel)**;
- or open a [discussion in GitHub Issues](https://github.com/shefelchana/genealogy-guide-ru/issues);
- or send a correction yourself (a pull request): the texts are under the CC BY 4.0 licence, the code under MIT.

Where else to look for like-minded people — [Other people's trees, communities, researchers](../2-reference/18-communities-researchers.md). Joint projects already under way that you can join as a volunteer (indexing, translations, shtetl pages, cemeteries) are in the section "Joint projects and hubs" of [Forums and communities](../../catalog/forums-and-communities.md). What else is not covered by region — at the end of the [Regional overview](../../catalog/regional-overview.md).
