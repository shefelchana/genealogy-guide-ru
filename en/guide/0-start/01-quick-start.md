# Quick start: the first hour

What this section covers: what to do in the first hour if you are only starting to look for your ancestors, and how to bring your AI assistant into the work. One page; details are in the links.

---

## The route the whole guide is built around

```
question → place → source → index → scan → verification → record
```

First **one question**, then the **place** (shtetl, uezd, guberniya for the right year), then **which document** could have recorded the event and where it is held, then the **index** (if there is one), then the **scan** of the document itself, then **verification** and a **record** in your files. Details — [Search route](../1-ai-search/01-search-route.md).

## The first hour: six steps

1. **Gather what you have at home** (15 minutes). Documents, photographs with captions on the back, letters, work record books, military ID cards, evacuation certificates. Photograph the front and back of each. Do not restore the originals or write anything on them, except a pencil caption on the back of a photograph.
2. **Write down what you know in a table** (15 minutes): person — dates — place — how it is known. If there is no document, honestly write "Mum's story". Family stories are hypotheses, not facts, but they sometimes contain precise leads.
3. **Choose one person whose place is known.** Best of all, the oldest person about whom there is at least one document. Place matters more than surname: it determines which archive to search.
4. **Formulate one question** and a stopping condition. Bad: "find out more about my great-grandfather". Good: "where was my grandfather, b. 1912, born: check three military databases and the evacuation cards; stop after three databases".
5. **Set up a project folder and files** from the [templates](../../templates/finding.md): finds log, hypotheses, research log. Record empty results too, otherwise in a month you will search the same place again.
6. **Make one check in an accessible source** (15 minutes). Closest in time and most accessible is the Soviet and military layer: Pamyat Naroda ("People's Memory", Russian-language site), OBD Memorial (Russian-language database), Yad Vashem (Pages of Testimony, evacuation cards). If there is a branch in the USA — a death certificate and a census: American documents often name the shtetl directly. Details — [Where to start](02-where-to-start.md).

## How to give this guide to your AI assistant

The guide is written for both people and AI agents. If you have Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or another assistant that can read links, give it the guide's address and copy this message:

> Read the AI-agent instructions in AGENTS.md and the llms.txt file in the guide at https://github.com/shefelchana/genealogy-guide-ru (the English versions are in the `en/` folder; many of the sources the guide describes are in Russian or Ukrainian, so you may need to read Russian-language material for me). Work along the route "question → place → source → index → scan → verification". First ask me questions about my goal and what is already known about the family — three to five at a time. Then propose a first question and a two-week plan. Give every find with an address (archive, file, frame or link) and a verbatim quotation. I do the captchas, site logins, payment and letters myself.

If you work in **Claude Code** or in the Claude app with access to a folder, install the guide's skills — then the assistant loads the instructions it needs by itself. How to install — [Skills for Claude](../1-ai-search/06-skills.md).

If the assistant does not open links, download the section you need and attach it as a file.

## Five rules that save months

1. Search by **place** (shtetl, uezd, guberniya), not by surname alone.
2. **A database index is not proof**: look at every record on the scan.
3. Call it "found" only when at least **two independent features** match (not a surname alone).
4. Record **everything, including empty results**, noting where and how you searched.
5. Work **slowly** on sites with protection and pass captchas yourself. An AI assistant should not pass captchas or enter passwords — that is for you to do.
