# Beginner mistakes: a consolidated list

What this section covers: sixteen typical mistakes of beginning genealogists — from the advice of well-known authors and communities — and where in the guide it is written how to avoid them.

Sources of the list:
- **Amy Johnson Crow**, "Genealogy Mistakes That Everyone Makes (and How to Avoid Them)", amyjohnsoncrow.com;
- **FamilySearch Wiki, "Rookie Mistakes"**;
- **Crista Cowan** (Ancestry) in the interview "How They Do It", organizeyourfamilyhistory.com;
- **V. V. Krasko**, an article in the collection "Genealogiya" (Genealogy) of the Russian Geographical Society (RGO), no. 3, 2003 (petergen.com/metod/sovkrasko.shtml, Russian-language);
- **petergen.com, "Genealogy mistakes that can damage your research"** (*Oshibki genealogii, kotorye mogut nanesti uron vashim issledovaniyam*; author not named; Russian-language);
- **the VGD forum**, docs.vgd.ru/kak-najti-predkov (Russian-language).

A short list from VGD's observations is in [Where to start](../0-start/03-where-to-start.md), section 7. Here is the full one.

## A few more mistakes from this guide

They are not in the lists above but grew out of the practice behind this guide:
- taking a **database index** for the document — [Standard of proof](01-standard-of-proof.md), section 6;
- searching by **surname** rather than by place — [Where to start](../0-start/03-where-to-start.md), section 4;
- believing an AI find without an address and quotation, without checking against the scan — [Checking results](../1-ai-search/04-checking-results.md);
- looking only at the year the family named rather than all surviving years of the book — [Records of the Russian Empire and the USSR](../2-reference/01-records-empire-ussr.md), section 2;
- paging through a file without an index when an index exists — [Community surname indexes](../2-reference/08-surname-indexes.md);
- searching for one ancestor instead of assembling the whole family — [Reconstructing the whole family](../2-reference/09-whole-family.md);
- believing an encyclopedia or Wikipedia in the "place of birth" field — [Standard of proof](01-standard-of-proof.md), section 1;
- paying for what lies in open access — [Access, captchas, pace](../2-reference/19-access-captchas-pace.md).
