# Groups and communities

What this section covers: where you can ask a question, find those who are looking for the same surnames and shtetls, and get help reading documents. The technical features of forums (j-roots, JGFF, ViewMate) are in [../catalog/forums-and-communities.md](../catalog/forums-and-communities.md).

The VGD forum considers trying to do everything alone a typical beginner's mistake. Communities help to read difficult handwriting, find "co-researchers" and learn about new digitizations.

## International (English)

- **Tracing the Tribe — Jewish Genealogy on Facebook** — about 78 thousand members per search results; an open group (anyone can read, members can write); moderator Schelly Talalay Dardashti.
- **JewishGen Discussion Group** (groups.jewishgen.org/g/main) — a mailing list with an archive since 1998; free registration.
- **Genealogy and Artificial Intelligence** (Facebook) — founded by Blaine Bettinger in 2023, about 7,000 members by May 2024 [checked]. See [ai-in-genealogy.md](ai-in-genealogy.md).
- **JewishGen Family Finder (JGFF)** — not a group but a "who is looking for what" database: enter all your surnames and shtetls.
- **ViewMate** — free help from volunteers with translating and reading scans.

### In Hebrew

- **The Tapuz forum "שורשים משפחתיים"** ("Family Roots", tapuz.co.il) — open without login, alive (posts of autumn 2026) [checked]. Recurring advice: write where you have already searched; search for "how it sounds" rather than the exact spelling; check Yad Vashem, Arolsen, USHMM and the Holocaust collections of Ancestry — they have different holdings; check the JewishGen Family Finder; state the country and region of the place.
- **IGRA** (genealogy.org.il) — the site is open, the databases after free registration; there is no separate forum; webinars in Hebrew and English [checked].
- **"קהילת חוקרות וחוקרי גנאלוגיה בישראל — The Israeli Genealogy Hub"** (Facebook) — the main Hebrew group, closed, about 4.3 thousand members (October 2026). Only the person himself or herself joins.
- A search by the Hebrew words "פורום גנאלוגיה" (genealogy forum), "חקר שורשים" (root research), "אילן יוחסין" (family tree) mostly gives commercial services and reference articles; there are few topical threads, almost all on Tapuz.

## Regional groups on Facebook

The names are from the catalog page jewishgenealogysurnameproject.com (the section "Jewish genealogy FB groups — country specific") [checked]; links to the groups themselves are not listed there — search by name.

- **Ukraine:** Jewish Ancestry in Ukraine; Jewish roots in Ukraine; Mogilev-Podolskiy Jewish Genealogy Group; Tulchin Folks; Jewish Ancestry in Berdichev; Jewish Ancestry in Lypovets; groups for Volhynia, Rivne, Kovel, Rohatyn and others; the Ukraine Research Division JewishGen group.
- **Bessarabia and Moldova:** Bessarabian / Moldavian Jewish Roots; Yedinitz/Edinet Genealogy Group; Jewish Memory — Jewish History and Genealogy in Moldova; Novoselitz.
- **Belarus:** Jewish Families from Belarus Circa 1330–1945; Belarus Jewish Genealogy and Ancestral Travel; groups for Pinsk, Shklov, Brest and others.
- **Lithuania and Latvia:** Litvak SIG; Jewish genealogy in Latvia and Lithuania; Litvak Portal; groups for Šiauliai, Kėdainiai, Žagarė and others.
- **Russia:** Russian Jewish Genealogy; The Jews of Saint Petersburg / Евреи Петербурга.
- **Poland:** Jewish Genealogy Poland, JRI-Poland, Polish Jewish Roots and dozens of groups for individual towns.

The sizes of some groups, visible in October 2026 when logged in: Jewish genealogy in Latvia and Lithuania — about 10 thousand (closed); Jewish Genealogy Poland — about 9.6 thousand (closed); Jewish Genealogy Belarus — about 6.8 thousand (open); Belarus / Lithuania Jewish History & Genealogy — about 19 thousand (open).

⚠️ Facebook is inconvenient for systematic searching: a search within a group shows only the beginnings of posts, and the answers are hidden in the comments. For techniques, forums with open text are better. On the other hand, the groups sometimes have live offers of help on the spot ("I'll be at the archive — I'll look"); the arrangement is always made by the person himself or herself.

## Jewish genealogical societies (JGS)

Local societies exist in many cities of the world; the list is at IAJGS (iajgs.org/membership/member-societies/). The Ukraine Research Division advises joining a local society. In Israel — IGRA and "Eilam" ([institutes-and-projects.md](institutes-and-projects.md)).

## How to use communities

- Before asking, search the group's archive — the answer is often already there.
- Attach a scan (or a link) and the archival reference, say what you have already tried.
- Offer something of your own: documents you have read, indexes, translations.
- A community's answer is a hint, not proof: check it against the document.
- How to formulate a question so that it gets answered — [Other people's trees, communities, researchers](../guide/2-reference/18-communities-researchers.md), section 2.
- Do not publish data of living people without their consent.
