# Groups and communities

What this section covers: where you can ask a question, find people searching for the same surnames and shtetls, and get help reading documents. The technical features of the forums (j-roots, JGFF, ViewMate) are in [forums-and-communities.md](../catalog/forums-and-communities.md).

The VGD forum considers trying to do everything alone a typical beginner's mistake. Communities help read a hard handwriting, find "co-researchers" and learn about new digitizations.

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified] and others) are explained in the [Glossary](../guide/0-start/06-glossary.md). Facebook does not show the size of a group without a login, so the sizes are mostly unverified.

If you read English only, jump to "International (English)" below. Don't read Russian? See [For English speakers](../guide/0-start/08-for-english-speakers.md).

## International (English)

- **Tracing the Tribe — Jewish Genealogy on Facebook** — an open group (everyone can read, members can write); in August 2025 almost 82 thousand members from more than 100 countries (per the IAJGS obituary). The group and the blog of the same name were founded by Schelly Talalay Dardashti; she died on 16 August 2025 [checked: IAJGS obituary]. Who runs the group now has not been verified.
- **JewishGen Discussion Group** (groups.jewishgen.org/g/main) — a mailing list with an archive since 1993 (more than 100,000 messages, 20–50 emails a day) [checked: jewishgen.org, the page may be out of date]; free registration. To join — the button "Apply For Membership", the first posts are approved by moderators; emails, a digest or a web forum. Activity by date is not visible without login (the group page answers 402 or requires JavaScript).
- **JewishGen regional groups:** Ukraine RD — alive, news of new records April–September 2026, 100–150 thousand records at a time [checked]; Bessarabia SIG — news February and June 2026 [checked]; LitvakSIG — its own forum (free account) [checked]; Gesher Galicia — a free mailing list through JewishGen, the journal and Family Finder — by membership from $50 [checked]; Belarus SIG — Help Desk; JRI-Poland — a mailing list with a moderator [from search snippet].
- **Jewish Genealogy Portal** (Facebook) — connected with JewishGen; 70–77 thousand members according to different directories [from search snippet].
- **Genealogical Translations** (Facebook) — a closed group of volunteer translators (Russian, Hebrew, Polish and others): one document a day, an answer usually within 48 hours [from search snippet].
- **Genealogy and Artificial Intelligence** (Facebook) — founded by Blaine Bettinger in 2023, by May 2024 about 7,000 members [checked]. See [ai-in-genealogy.md](ai-in-genealogy.md).
- **JewishGen Family Finder (JGFF)** — not a group, but a "who is looking for what" database: enter all your surnames and shtetls.
- **ViewMate** — free help from volunteers with translating and reading scans.

### In Hebrew

- **The Tapuz forum "שורשים משפחתיים"** ("Family roots", tapuz.co.il) — open without login, alive: the topic list has dates 04–27.09.2026, several topics a week [checked, 08.10.2026]. Recurring advice: write where you have already searched; search "as it sounds", not the exact spelling; check Yad Vashem, Arolsen, USHMM and the Holocaust collections of Ancestry — they have different holdings; check the JewishGen Family Finder; state the country and region of the place.
- **IGRA** (genealogy.org.il) — the site is open, the databases after free registration; there is no separate forum; webinars in Hebrew and English [checked]. In-person meetings in autumn 2026 — in Givatayim and Ra'anana (06.10, 18.10, 03.11), a webinar in English — 09.11 [from search snippet].
- **"קהילת חוקרות וחוקרי גנאלוגיה בישראל — The Israeli Genealogy Hub"** (Facebook) — the main Hebrew group, closed, about 4.3 thousand members (October 2026). Only the person themselves can join.
- A search for the Hebrew words "פורום גנאלוגיה", "חקר שורשים", "אילן יוחסין" mostly returns commercial services and reference articles; there are few thematic threads, almost all on Tapuz.

The sizes of some groups seen in October 2026 when logged in: Jewish genealogy in Latvia and Lithuania — about 10 thousand (closed); Jewish Genealogy Poland — about 9.6 thousand (closed); Jewish Genealogy Belarus — about 6.8 thousand (open); Belarus / Lithuania Jewish History & Genealogy — about 19 thousand (open).

⚠️ Facebook is inconvenient for systematic search: the search results in a group show only the beginnings of posts, and the answers are hidden in the comments. For techniques, forums with open text are better. On the other hand, groups sometimes have live offers of help on the spot ("I'll be at the archive — I'll look"); the person always makes the arrangement themselves.

## Jewish genealogical societies (JGS)

Local societies exist in many cities of the world; the list is at IAJGS (iajgs.org/membership/member-societies/), the map — iajgs.org/membership/jgs-world-map/: more than 90 societies, none of them Russian or Ukrainian [checked]. JGS Greater Boston runs separate groups on Ukraine, Belarus and Bessarabia [from search snippet]. The Ukraine Research Division advises joining a local society. In Israel — IGRA and Eilam ([institutes-and-projects.md](institutes-and-projects.md)).

## How to use communities

- Before asking, search the group's archive — the answer is often already there.
- Attach a scan (or a link) and the archival reference, say what you have already tried.
- Offer your own: documents you have read, indexes, translations.
- A community's answer is a hint, not proof: check it against the document.
- How to frame a question so that it gets answered — [Other people's trees, communities, researchers](../guide/2-reference/18-communities-researchers.md), section 2.
- Do not publish data of living people without their consent.
- If you want to help yourself — index, translate, run a shtetl page: the section "Joint projects and hubs" in [Forums and communities](../catalog/forums-and-communities.md).
