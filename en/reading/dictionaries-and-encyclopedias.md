# Dictionaries, encyclopedias, reference works

What this section covers: dictionaries of surnames and given names (to check a reading and find variants), encyclopedias (the history of shtetls and communities), memorial books and aids on old orthography. Place directories and libraries are in [directories-and-books.md](../catalog/directories-and-books.md).

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified] and others) are explained in the [Glossary](../guide/0-start/06-glossary.md). Language is given where the Russian text states it.

---

## Surnames and given names

| Resource | What it is and what it is good for | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Beider's surname dictionaries** + the index stevemorse.org/phonetics/beider.php | The index searches a surname at once in Beider's four dictionaries (Russian Empire 2008, Kingdom of Poland 1996, Galicia 2004, Prague 1994) and in Mencke's dictionary, with phonetic matching (Beider-Morse, Daitch-Mokotoff); it shows in which dictionary the surname is [checked]. **Not in the dictionaries — almost certainly a misreading** | the index is free, the books are paid; English |
| **Steve Morse One-Step** (stevemorse.org) | Russian ↔ English transliteration, conversion of Jewish-calendar dates, Soundex, old telephone books of Moscow and St Petersburg, NKVD employees 1935–1939 [checked] | free |
| **Beider, "Handbook of Ashkenazic Given Names and Their Variants"** (2009) | 15,000 variants from 735 root names | paid |
| **Feldblyum, "Russian-Jewish Given Names"** (1998) | more than 6,000 names (after Kulisher's work of 1911) | paid |
| **Consolidated Jewish Surname Index** (Avotaynu) | the surname index of the Avotaynu publisher | avotaynu.com (terms of access not checked; the CSI service returned a server error in September 2026) |
| **JewAge** (jewage.org) | articles on the origin of surnames, a common tree | see [institutes-and-projects.md](institutes-and-projects.md) |

## Places and communities

- **JewishGen Communities Database / Gazetteer** — more than 6,000 communities with name variants; about 2.4 million localities [checked].
- **Where Once We Walked** (Mokotoff & Sack) — more than 23,500 shtetls and 17,500 alternative names [checked: avotaynu.com, 2026-10-07]; paid.
- **KehilaLinks** (kehilalinks.jewishgen.org, the Ukraine index — /ukraine.html) — shtetl sites [from search snippet].
- **Directories of administrative division** (an example for Podolia): "Lists of inhabited places of Podolia guberniya… by Jewish societies" (prlib.ru/item/466841), the ATD (administrative-territorial division) section of the Presidential Library (prlib.ru/section/1155766), the uezds and volosts of Podolia on genealogy-ua.com, "List of inhabited places of Podolia gub. 1905" on familio.org [from search snippet].

## Memorial (yizkor) books

- **JewishGen Yizkor Book Project** (jewishgen.org/yizkor, since 1994): translations, a bibliographic database, the Necrology Index, the Master Name Index [checked].
- **New York Public Library**: more than 800 books, about 85% of them online (together with the Yiddish Book Center) [checked: libguides.nypl.org/yizkorbooks, 2026-10-07]; an index by town is there too; PDFs — yiddishbookcenter.org/collections/yizkor-books.
- Full-text search across roughly half of the books — Genealogy Indexer [from search snippet].

## Pre-reform orthography

- The Wikipedia article on pre-reform orthography (yat, fita, izhitsa, i, the final hard sign); the dictionary dorev.ru; the aid "Introduction to pre-reform orthography" (Введение в дореформенную орфографию; imwerden.de/pdf/winitsky_vvedenie_v_orfografiju.pdf) [from search snippet].
- When searching digitized books try **both spellings** (see [Names, surnames, spellings](../guide/2-reference/03-names-spellings.md)).

## Guides

- **Center for Jewish History**: "Basic Resources", "Finding Your Ancestral Town", guides to Ukraine, Lithuania, Israel (cjh.org/pdfs/BasicResources.pdf, cjh.org/pdfs/Ukraine.pdf) [from search snippet]; free, PDF, English.
- **FamilySearch Research Wiki** — the pages "Ukraine Jewish Records", "Moldova Jewish Records", "Belarus Jewish Records", "Russia Jewish Records", "Jewish Genealogy Research" [checked].
- **IGRA**, the handout "Ukraine 2020" (genealogy.org.il/files/IGRAUkraine2020HANDOUT-FINAL.pdf) [checked].
