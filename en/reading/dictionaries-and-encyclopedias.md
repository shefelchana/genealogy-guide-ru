# Dictionaries, encyclopedias, reference works

What this section covers: dictionaries of surnames and given names (to check a reading and find variants), encyclopedias (the history of shtetls and communities), memorial books and aids on old spelling. Place directories and libraries are in [../catalog/directories-and-books.md](../catalog/directories-and-books.md).

Markers: **[checked]** — the page was read (October 2026); **[from search snippet]** — information from search results.

---

## Surnames and given names

| Resource | What it is and how it helps | Access |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Beider's surname dictionaries** + the index stevemorse.org/phonetics/beider.php | The index looks up a surname in all four Beider dictionaries at once (Russian Empire 2008, Kingdom of Poland 1996, Galicia 2004, Prague 1994) and in Menk's dictionary, with phonetic matching (Beider-Morse, Daitch-Mokotoff); it shows in which dictionary the surname occurs [checked]. **If it is not in the dictionaries, it is almost certainly a misreading** | the index is free, the books are paid; English |
| **Steve Morse One-Step** (stevemorse.org) | Russian ↔ English transliteration, conversion of Jewish calendar dates, Soundex, old telephone directories of Moscow and St. Petersburg, NKVD employees 1935–1939 [checked] | free |
| **Beider, "Handbook of Ashkenazic Given Names and Their Variants"** (2009) | 15,000 variants from 735 root names | paid |
| **Feldblyum, "Russian-Jewish Given Names"** (1998) | over 6,000 names (after Kulisher's work of 1911) | paid |
| **Consolidated Jewish Surname Index** (Avotaynu) | the surname index of the Avotaynu publisher | avotaynu.com (access conditions not verified; the CSI service gave a server error in September 2026) |
| **JewAge** (jewage.org) | articles on the origin of surnames, a general tree | see [institutes-and-projects.md](institutes-and-projects.md) |

## Places and communities

- **JewishGen Communities Database / Gazetteer** — over 6,000 communities with name variants; about 2.4 million localities [checked].
- **Where Once We Walked** (Mokotoff & Sack) — over 23,500 shtetls and 17,500 alternative names [page: avotaynu.com, 2026-10-07]; paid.
- **KehilaLinks** (kehilalinks.jewishgen.org, index for Ukraine — /ukraine.html) — shtetl sites [from search snippet].
- **Directories of administrative division** (an example for Podolia): "Spiski naselennykh mest Podolskoi gubernii… po evreiskim obshchestvam" (Lists of populated places of Podolia guberniya… by Jewish communities; prlib.ru/item/466841), the ATD (administrative-territorial division) section of the Presidential Library (prlib.ru/section/1155766), uezds and volosts of Podolia on genealogy-ua.com, "Spisok naselennykh mest Podolskoi gub. 1905" (List of populated places of Podolia guberniya 1905) on familio.org [from search snippet]. (Russian-language)

## Memorial books (yizkor)

- **JewishGen Yizkor Book Project** (jewishgen.org/yizkor, since 1994): translations, a bibliographic database, the Necrology Index, the Master Name Index [checked].
- **New York Public Library**: over 800 books, about 85% of them online (together with the Yiddish Book Center) [page: libguides.nypl.org/yizkorbooks, 2026-10-07]; an index by town is there too; PDFs — yiddishbookcenter.org/collections/yizkor-books.
- Full-text search across roughly half of the books — Genealogy Indexer [from search snippet].

## Pre-reform spelling

- The Wikipedia article on pre-reform Russian orthography (yat, fita, izhitsa, i, the final hard sign); the dictionary dorev.ru; the manual "Vvedenie v dorevolyutsionnuyu orfografiyu" (Introduction to pre-reform spelling; imwerden.de/pdf/winitsky_vvedenie_v_orfografiju.pdf) [from search snippet]. (Russian-language)
- When searching digitized books try **both spellings** (see [Names, surnames, spellings](../guide/2-reference/03-names-spellings.md)).
