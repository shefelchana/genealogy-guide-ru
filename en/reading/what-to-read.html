What to read and where to learn: by level

What this section covers: books, free courses, webinars, journals and training programs — from the first steps to professional certification. Divided into three levels: beginner, experienced, specialist. Authors, institutes and dictionaries already described in the guide are only named here, with a link.

The markers in square brackets ([checked], [from search snippet], [unverified]) are explained in the Glossary. Checked 08.10.2026. "Where to get it": free / buy / library. Prices and enrollment dates change fast — check on the site before paying. English-language items come first within each level wherever the order does not matter; Russian-language ones are marked "(in Russian)".
