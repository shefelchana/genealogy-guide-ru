# About the compiler

Hi! I'm Anna Shefel. I first got to know archives while gathering documents for my aliyah (immigration to Israel). But it's not only about paperwork: knowing my family's story matters to me, and I've been working on it bit by bit for many years. Right now I'm reconstructing two lines: the Shefels from Podolia and the Weinbergs from Bessarabia.

I'm not a professional genealogist, just an enthusiast. I rely on my own knowledge and on openly available information.

**What I use**
- **Claude (Claude Code)** — an AI assistant and agents that read scans, try out surname spellings, search databases and keep a research log. **Gemini** — for a second opinion.
- **Scans and databases:** FamilySearch, JewishGen, Wikimedia Commons (files from Ukrainian archives), Yad Vashem, newspapers of the National Library of Israel, the "Jewish Roots" forum (j-roots, Russian-language).
- **Tree** — MyHeritage. **DNA** — 23andMe, FamilyTreeDNA, GEDmatch.

This guide is the way of working that has taken shape for me, plus the experience of genealogists and communities I cite throughout.
