- Person: [full name in the spelling of the era and in the modern one], [year/date], [place: shtetl, uezd, guberniya]

— A targeted request (exact archival reference, folio, record number) is processed faster and more cheaply than a thematic one ("find everything about the family").
— Language: the language of the archive's country (Ukrainian, Romanian, Russian, Polish…); English as a fallback.
