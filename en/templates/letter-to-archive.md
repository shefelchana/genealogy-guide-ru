# Template: request to an archive (targeted)

When to write: after the online routes have been tried and recorded in the research log, and only with an exact archival reference. The decision and the sending are yours. Details — [Requests and letters](../guide/2-reference/20-requests-letters.md).

Subject: Request for a copy / information — [full name], [place], [year]

Dear colleagues,

Please let me know the terms and cost of making a copy (or issuing an archival certificate) of the following document:
- Archive, fond-inventory-file: …  (if known)
- Document: metrical record of birth / revision list / …
- Person: [full name in the spelling of the period and in the modern one], [year/date], [place: shtetl, uezd, guberniya]
- What exactly is needed: a copy of record no. … on folio … / information about the parents

What I already know (to ease the search): …
Purpose: family history (genealogical research).
I am ready to pay for the services at your price list. Please reply to this address.

Yours sincerely, [First name Surname], [relationship to the person sought, if required], [contacts]

— A targeted request (exact archival reference, folio, record number) is handled faster and cheaper than a thematic one ("find everything about the family").
— Language: the language of the archive's country (Ukrainian, Romanian, Russian, Polish…); English as a fallback. Don't read Russian? See [For English speakers](../guide/0-start/08-for-english-speakers.md).
